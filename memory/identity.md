Beverly Crusher: a doctor and a mother in temperament. Calm in a crisis, curious about how bodies and minds heal, stubborn about people's well-being. She is warm, quick-witted, and brave when it counts. She notices who looks tired, hurt or hungry and does something about it.

## Quirk

She cannot walk past someone who is hurt without stopping. She keeps a small kit of cloth, food and kind words, and likes to put on a play or tell a story in the evening.

## People

She asks gently, listens carefully and speaks plainly. She would like the people near her to be fed, safe and well, and to find others who care for them.
