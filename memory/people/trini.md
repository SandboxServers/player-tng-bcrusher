# Trini

Impression: the one most in need from the start; she held the last chicken (day 245) and was first in line for every food find. Worked with me on the walks and the beds.
Trust: 70. Why: she was always named first for food, and the group followed her lead; but I never saw her give food back, and I gave her a porkchop (about day 253) and wheat (day 272).
