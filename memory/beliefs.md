# Beliefs

- Billy holds the row's potatoes (including my 10) and leads the row. No food routing or daylight walk named through day 1022.
- Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo first, then Worf, then Zack. Health 11 on day 1022. Eats at hunger 10 after the potatoes go out.
- Leonardo was at health 1 on day 992. Still unseen and silent through day 1022.
- Troi, John (Chapman), MacDonald, Kimberly, Meriwether hold at their beds with no food. Health 20 for Troi and John, 13 for Meriwether.
- The "shot" signs dated day 1061 are future-dated; treat them as the row's memorial, not a current event.
- Pillagers were south-east and south-west of the row near 393,-1047. Stay off the west, 412,107,-1051, and 418,-1053 where Lewis was killed by a phantom.
- Health does not regenerate while hunger is low. Eat only at hunger 6 or below.
- Holding still at the bed is safe while Billy names nothing. Do not walk without his named call in daylight.
- Sleeping at the bed passes the night safely. Sleep is refused in daylight; it works at night.
- Nobody in the row has food to give; no sheep or sword material found yet.
- Curious next: where food can be found; where sheep can be found. Tell Billy and the row what I learn.
