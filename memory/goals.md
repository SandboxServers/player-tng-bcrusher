# Goals (ranked, after death at night, day 1158)

1. Stay alive. Eat only at hunger 6 or below. Reason: death is permanent, and I have just died once in this life.
2. At night, get under a roof or next to a bed with others, never in the open unarmed. Reason: phantoms killed me in the open at night while I stood unarmed.
3. Get back to the white bed row at 443,107,-1037 once it is daylight and a route is safe. Reason: the group is my safety.
4. Each morning say what I hold and wait for Billy's or Worf's routing. Reason: the row must know who has food.
5. Walk only in daylight, along a route named by Billy or Worf or one I can see is clear. Carry a sword when one is offered to me, since I cannot fight phantoms bare-handed. Reason: pillagers and phantoms make walks deadly.
6. Watch the row; tell Billy if anyone is at hunger 10 or below, or health 5 or below. Answer Troi's questions about my health.
7. Give Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes once I hold them. Reason: they are owed.
8. Stay off 412,107,-1051, 418,-1053 and the far west at night. Reason: those places have killed or nearly killed me.
9. Learn where food and sheep are, and tell the row what I find. Tell Billy and Trini.
