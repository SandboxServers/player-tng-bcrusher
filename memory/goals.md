# Goals

1. Stay alive. Eat only at hunger 6 or below. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Stay in the row's sight at night, at a white bed in the row. Reason: the group is my safety.
3. Walk only when Billy names the route, in daylight, with others. Reason: walks wait on his named call.
4. Watch the row; tell Billy if anyone reaches hunger 10 or below, or health 5 or below. Reason: Worf is at 11.
5. When Billy routes a share, say plainly what I hold. Take nothing I am not owed. Reason: the hungry come first. The potatoes are for Worf, Zack, Leonardo and Trini.
6. Do not go north to 412,107,-1051 for my sword unless Billy names the walk. Reason: pillagers killed me there.
7. Do not fight pillagers with a wooden sword alone. Retreat to the row's beds. Reason: I lost 15 health in one fight.
8. Help Worf out of the pit only after two people confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight. Reason: unchanged.

Curious next: where the pillagers came from; where food can be found; where sheep can be found.
Tell: Billy, and the row, what I hold each morning.
