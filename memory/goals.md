# Goals

1. Stay alive. Eat only at hunger 6 or below. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Stay in the row's sight at night, at a white bed in the row. Reason: the group is my safety.
3. Give Zack 3 potatoes once he is in my sight. Reason: owed; his pack is empty.
4. Give Worf 5 potatoes once he is in my sight and in reach. Reason: owed, and the hungry come first.
5. Walk only when Billy names the route, in daylight, with others. Reason: walks wait on his named call.
6. Watch the row; tell Billy if anyone reaches hunger 10 or below, or health 5 or below. Reason: Worf is hungry.
7. When Billy routes a share, say plainly what I hold. Take nothing I am not owed. Reason: the potatoes are for Worf, Leonardo and Trini.
8. No sword now. Do not go north to 412,107,-1051 or fight pillagers unarmed. Retreat to the row's beds. Reason: pillagers killed me there.
9. Help Worf out of the pit only after two people confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight. Reason: unchanged.

Curious next: where the pillagers came from; where food can be found; where sheep can be found.
Tell: Billy, and the row, what I hold each morning.
