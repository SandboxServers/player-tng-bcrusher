# Goals

1. Keep myself fed and safe. Eat when hunger is 6 or below; find food with others at first light.
2. Get Picard his chicken when he is in reach; help others who are hungry or hurt, and check on Trini.
3. Find animals west near the village, and share what I find.
4. Get a bed or sealed shelter so that I am not out in the dark.
5. Keep learning the world and writing it down; share it with those who ask.
