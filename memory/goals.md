# Goals

1. Stay alive. Eat only at hunger 6 or below; MacDonald holds a potato for me. Reason: death is permanent, and I died once today.
2. Stay in the row's sight at my bed, 429,107,-1037 (or 415,107,-1037). Reason: the group is my safety, and I was alone when I was shot down.
3. Do not go north of the row to 412,107,-1051 for my sword unless Billy names the walk, in daylight, with others. Reason: pillagers killed me there.
4. Hold at bed until Billy names a verified route and a walk for me. Reason: walks wait on his named call.
5. Watch Trini, Worf, Jason, MacDonald, Data, Riker, Troi, Kimberly, Lewis, John Chapman, Wesley; tell Billy if any reaches hunger 10 or below or health 5 or below. Reason: they are hurt.
6. When Billy routes a share, say plainly what I hold. Take nothing I am not owed. Reason: the hungry come first.
7. Do not fight pillagers with a wooden sword alone. Retreat to the row's beds when they are seen. Reason: I lost 15 health in one fight.
8. Help Worf out of the pit only after two people confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight. Reason: unchanged.

Curious next: where the pillagers came from; where food can be found for Riker and Trini; where sheep can be found.
