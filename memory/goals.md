1. Stay alive. Eat only at hunger 6 or below. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Sleep at my white bed 443,107,-1037 each night, in the row's sight. Reason: the group is my safety.
3. Each morning say what I hold and wait at the bed for Billy's or Worf's routing. Reason: the row must know who has food.
4. Walk only when Billy or Worf names the route or cell, in daylight, with a sword in sight. Reason: pillagers make walks deadly.
5. Watch the row; tell Billy if anyone is at hunger 10 or below, or health 5 or below. Answer Troi's questions about my health.
6. Give Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes once I hold them, or once Billy routes them to me. Reason: they are owed.
7. No sword; stay off 412,107,-1051, 418,-1053 and the west.
8. Learn where food and sheep are, and tell the row what I find. Curious next; tell Billy and Trini.
