# Day 1082
Quiet day at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. I held nothing and hunger stayed at 17, health 19. No routing from Billy and no walk was named, so I did not leave the bed. I said my holdings in the morning and again later. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack, with Leonardo first. Meriwether, Kimberly, Chapman, MacDonald and Deanna are all holding at their beds too. I slept at my bed at night.
