# Day 1082

Quiet day. I woke at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17 (not eating until 6). No walks were named and Billy gave no routing. Lewis and Chapman spoke from their beds at night, so the row is holding. I said my position aloud and slept at my bed. Still owed: Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes; Trini holds 2 at bed 430. Next morning: say what I hold, wait at the bed for Billy's routing.
