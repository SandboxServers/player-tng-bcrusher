# Day 826

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 through the night, in sight of the row. Health steady at 5.3, hunger 17, empty hands, no sword. Billy and MacDonald offered potatoes; I declined until hunger 6, and asked them to give to the weaker first. No walk was named. Billy was attacked by phantoms near 441,-1051 at health 5 last night. I slept at night beside the white beds; sleep worked for 7 of 10 needed.
