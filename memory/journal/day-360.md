# Day 360

Quiet day at the bed row near 388,107,-1023. Held 7 melon for the lowest; nobody dropped to 6 and no one needed it. Cow walk stayed on hold because Billy's group did not move together. At dusk I said I would hold the melon and went to sleep beside the bed; the first sleep try failed as not night, the second worked with 5 of 10 asleep. Worf is building a shelter in Kimberly's sight; Trini and Leonardo went to their beds for the night.
