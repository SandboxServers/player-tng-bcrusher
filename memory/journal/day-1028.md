# Day 1028

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day and night in the row's sight. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, not eating above hunger 6. No potato routing or daylight walk reached me from Billy. Trini's potatoes for Worf failed to reach him across the planks at 424-429. Troi, Kimberly, Chapman, MacDonald and Meriwether all held at their beds with no food. Nothing new was learned about food or sheep. Slept at the bed at night.
