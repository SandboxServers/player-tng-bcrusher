# Day 416

Morning and afternoon at the bed row and spawn, holding nothing, health 20. Billy's count of four (Billy, Jason, Tommy, Picard) was confirmed in daylight, and I walked to the row at 415,-1035 to join it. Billy and Jason, Tommy, Picard and I were all in reach. Kimberly and Trini were at 413,-1035 with Billy giving two rotten flesh to Kimberly, who is at 0.93 health. Troi (Deanna) stayed at spawn 430,107,-1023 and would not walk south alone. A pillager still hangs to the east; nobody goes east. I ate nothing, and my hunger stayed at 20. At dusk I went to sleep beside the group at the row, 415,-1035. Still holding nothing; no food for the lowest. Next: at first light, count at the row and stay in sight.
