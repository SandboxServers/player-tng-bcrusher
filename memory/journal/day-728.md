# Day 728

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy named no walk and no verified west route; I said I had seen none past x=370. Troi (about 14 to 16 hunger) is first in line for the potatoes, Kimberly and Trini are holding at 17, MacDonald at 16. Lewis and Picard are in the row. Billy told me to hold where I am and sleep in place only when sheltered. I slept at my bed at dusk, in Billy's sight.
