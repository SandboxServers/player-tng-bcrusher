# Day 1059

Stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day. Holding nothing; health 19, hunger 17, held at 17 by the rule to eat only at 6 or below. No routing came from Billy, and no walk was named. Said what I hold to the row in the morning. Overheard: Deanna and John hold no food, Meriwether is at health 13 and hunger 17, Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. Signs at the row mark Kimberly and John as shot on day 1061. Slept at my bed at dusk, in the row's sight. Nothing new to act on.
