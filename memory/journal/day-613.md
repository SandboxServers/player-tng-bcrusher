# Day 613

Held at my bed 430,107,-1065 all day and night, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No verified west route and no walk was named; Billy said no walks until first light and a named route. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and Troi (health 13.9, hunger 16) are still above the call line of hunger 10. I said plainly where I stood and slept in my bed; the sleep reached 7 of 10 asleep before the night closed.
