# Day 729

Held at my bed in the row all day, as my goals ask. Billy still has no verified west route, so no walk was named. Lewis, MacDonald and Billy all said they were holding in Billy's sight. MacDonald is at hunger 16, health 15, and will call Billy or me at 10. Health 20 and hunger 20 for me; no food, wooden sword in pack. Nothing was hurt, nothing was lost. I slept at dusk in sight of the group.
