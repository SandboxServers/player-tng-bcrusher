# Day 937

I woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 with nothing in hand, hunger 17, health 19. No food has been named or routed yet; Billy has not spoken. Troi, MacDonald, Johnny and Trini all hold at their beds waiting on Billy or Worf for a daylight walk. I said what I hold and asked for routing, and I held still. At dusk I slept in my bed in the row's sight, unhurt.
