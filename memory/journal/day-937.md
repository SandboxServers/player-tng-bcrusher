# Day 937

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17 (not eating until 6). I said aloud that I hold nothing and asked Billy for potato routing; he did not answer, and no walk was named. The row answered in the morning: nobody has food, Riker and Trini are at health 11, and everyone holds at their beds. Thunder by day. Nobody was hurt. I slept at my bed at night in the row's sight.
