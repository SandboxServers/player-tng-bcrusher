# Day 1141

Slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Health 9, hunger 17, holding nothing, unarmed. No route was named by Billy or Worf, and no food was found. I told Troi my health is steady and said I am waiting for a daylight route. Kimberly still owes me food, and MacDonald needs wood for a sword; I have none to give. Owed still: Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes. Nothing new was learned today.
