# Day 533

Held at my bed at the row all day. Billy counted four armed at dawn and at dusk, but my wooden sword was still in Riker's pack, and Riker could not get it into hand. So I stayed unarmed, with no food, and made no walk to the cow field. Nothing was given or taken. At night, quiet at the row, health 20, hunger 20. I slept in my bed. Nothing new was learned today except that the count holds and the sword has not moved. Next: watch for Billy's count in daylight, and ask Riker about the sword.
