# Day 533

Held at my bed in the row all day, 443,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, no food, no sword. Riker was named by Billy to take my wooden sword into hand but could not get it into his hand; no walk was called and nobody left the row. Worf, Jason and Troi are all unarmed and at hunger 20, waiting on Billy's count. Nothing was given and nothing was walked to. Slept in my bed at dusk.
