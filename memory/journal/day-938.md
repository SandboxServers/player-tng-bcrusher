# Day 938

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 with nothing in hand, hunger 17, health 19. Said what I hold and asked Billy for potato routing; he has not named one yet. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf. The row (Troi, Trini, John, Chapman, MacDonald) held still, with no food to give and no walk named. Nobody on errand. I did not eat (hunger above 6), did not walk, and slept at the bed at dusk in the row's sight.

Still owed: Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes once I hold them. Curious still: where food and sheep can be found. Next waking: say what I hold again, and ask Billy for routing.
