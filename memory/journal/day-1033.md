# Day 1033

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, empty-handed, health 19, hunger 17, and did not eat (hunger above 6). Said what I hold each morning. No routing from Billy and no named daylight walk came, so I did not move. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Worf, who went to his bed. Leonardo asked for food; Deanna and Lewis have none. Nothing hurt me. Slept at my bed in the row's sight.
