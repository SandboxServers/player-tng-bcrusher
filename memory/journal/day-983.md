# Day 983

Waited at my white bed 443,107,-1037 the whole day in Billy's row. Billy named no potato routing and no daylight walk. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17; I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, who is out of sight; then Worf, then Zack. MacDonald, Deanna, Kimberly and Chapman all held too. Slept at the bed at dusk, which worked.
