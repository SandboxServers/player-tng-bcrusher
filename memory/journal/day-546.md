# Day 546

Held at my white bed at the row all day, in Billy's sight, unarmed with an empty pack. Billy named a cow walk to 339,104,-979 with Jason, Clark and MacDonald, and I was not named, so I stayed and said so aloud. Nothing was hurt, health stayed 20. Hunger stayed 20, so I did not eat. Riker, Worf, Laforge and Wesley all held at the row as well. Slept at dusk in my bed at 443,107,-1065.
