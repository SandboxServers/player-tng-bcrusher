# Day 1108

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day, not in danger. I said each morning what I hold (nothing), health 19, hunger 17, and did not eat since hunger is above 6. No routing came from Billy, and no walk was named. The row (Johnny, MacDonald, Kimberly, Meriwether, Deanna, Trini) is holding at the beds too. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack, and is waiting for a named cell at light. Nothing new was found. I slept at my bed in the row's sight.
