# Day 1213

Held at the bed row all day with the group in sight, nobody alone. Said what I hold each morning: 1 bread, 2 arrows, 2 bone, health 20, hunger 19. Trini named a north-east leg to -936,73,-3062 with Zack and Adam. I went into her reach as asked and my follow broke when she went out of sight, so I held at the row. She finished the leg, found drops 7 to 8 blocks east, and walked back with Zack and Adam. Nobody took food from me; I ate nothing, since hunger is 19. Sattler still holds 3 melon owed to Gimli, Chewie and Mary. I slept at the row in the group's sight, not enclosed.
