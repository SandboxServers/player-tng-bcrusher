# Day 1150

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day, with no route named. Nothing to eat, nothing in my pack, unarmed. Health 9, hunger 17, so I did not eat. Troi, Kimberly, MacDonald and Chapman all held too. Troi reports Trini has potatoes at 430,107,-1051, in the west, which I must not walk to. I slept at my white bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
