# Day 1143

Night at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Health 9, hunger 17, holding nothing, unarmed. No route named, no food found. Kimberly and Troi sleep beside me; the row held. I said goodnight to the row and slept at my bed. Nothing new to act on yet. Owed still: Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes once I hold them.
