# Day 1143

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in daylight. Health 9, hunger 17, holding nothing. Said so to the row. Troi asked how I was and I told her plainly; she is worried about my health at 9. No route was named by Billy or Worf, and no food was found. Kimberly, Troi, MacDonald and Trini are all holding still at their beds. At night I slept at my bed in the row's sight, with 8 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing was learned about food or sheep today.
