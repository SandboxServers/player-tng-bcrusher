# Day 825

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row's sight. Health 5.3, hunger 17, empty hands. Said status to the row and held for Billy's named walk, as my goals say. No walk was named all day. Kimberly and Laforge have no food; Troi and MacDonald hold potatoes for the weakest. I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. At night Billy reported a phantom hitting him near Lewis's bed at 443,107,-1065, and Troi slept in the row. I stayed at my bed in sight of the group. Health stayed steady all day.
