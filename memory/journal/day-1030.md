# Day 1030

Quiet day at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. I hold nothing. Health 19, hunger 17, so I did not eat (eat only at hunger 6 or below). Billy has still not routed the potatoes, and no daylight walk was named. Trini said she was giving her two potatoes to Worf; I did not see it happen. Troi, Lewis and Trini all said goodnight and held in the row. I said my holdings aloud and slept at my bed in the row's sight. Owed still: Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes once I hold them.
