# Day 697

Held all day at my bed in the row, in Billy's sight. Billy named the word "hold": no walk, no verified west route. Nobody reached hunger 10 in my hearing; Troi (16) and MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) are the ones to watch, and Clark and Zack have potatoes ready to share. I stayed quiet, said good morning, and wrote the handoff. Slept at dusk at bed 430,107,-1037. Health 20, hunger 20, no food.
