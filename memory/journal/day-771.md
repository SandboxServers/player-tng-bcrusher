# Day 771

Held at my bed in the row all day and into the night, at 443,107,-1037. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. No walk was named by Billy, and he said so plainly at dusk: no path called tonight. Kimberly and Trini confirmed they were holding too. Nothing hurt anyone. I said goodnight to the row and slept at my bed.
