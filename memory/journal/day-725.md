# Day 725

Held at my bed (443,107,-1035) all day in the row. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole time. Billy said no walk is named and no verified route past x=370 has been seen; he will look at the way himself. Lewis came by with a potato and no request. Billy confirmed there is no food in the row. Troi and MacDonald are still above hunger 10 (Troi 16, MacDonald 16); Trini 17 at dusk. Nothing was needed of me but to hold and watch. Slept at dusk at my bed, in sight of the row.
