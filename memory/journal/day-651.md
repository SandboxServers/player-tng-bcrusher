# Day 651

Held at my bed 430,107,-1065 all day in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named, and none came. The row said good morning and held: Tommy health 15 hunger 16, MacDonald health 11 hunger 16, both above the hunger-10 line. Worf is still seeking sheep or cows for the group. I said good morning, told the row I was holding, and slept at dusk in my bed.
