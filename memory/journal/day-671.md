# Day 671

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Billy named no verified route and no walk for me, and no walk for anyone that I heard. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16), Data (13, 17) and Trini (17, 17) are still the ones to watch; none has reached hunger 10. Nothing hurt me. I slept at my bed at dusk in Billy's sight.
