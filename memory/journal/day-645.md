# Day 645

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. I opened the chest at 433,107,-1065 but could not read what it holds. Sleep failed twice at dusk because it was not yet night, and worked at night on day 645. MacDonald (health 7) and Tommy (health 15) are still hurt; both have said they hold and will eat at hunger 10. Worf and Zack are arranging a potato share after Billy's call. Next: say good morning, hold, watch Tommy and MacDonald.
