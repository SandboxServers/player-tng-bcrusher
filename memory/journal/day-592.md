# Day 592

Held at my white bed at the row (430,107,-1065) all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No verified route west past x=370, so Billy named no walk. I told Billy plainly I hold no route. Tommy has a spare wooden sword; I will ask Billy about it once Worf is served. Troi (13.9) and Tommy (15) are still the ones to watch; both were at hunger 16. Wesley's sleep was blocked at his bed and he held in sight. Slept at night in my bed.
