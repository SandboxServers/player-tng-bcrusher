# Day 850

I held at my bed in the row all day. Health steady at 7.3, hunger 16, so I did not eat. I told the row my state and kept the 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini, to hand over only when Billy routes it and they are in sight. Billy named no walk, so I did not leave. At dusk Jason, Lewis and Worf settled near their beds, and I slept at mine. Nothing new was found today; the sheep and the source of the pillagers are still unknown.
