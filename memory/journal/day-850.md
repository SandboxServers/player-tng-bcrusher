# Day 850

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row all day. Told the row my state each morning: health 7.3, hunger 16, not eating. I hold 10 potatoes that Billy routed for Leonardo and Trini; they did not come into sight, so they stayed with me. Billy named no walk. Zack asked for a bed at 430,107,-1050 once Billy names it, with Worf or Jason in sight. At dusk I slept at my bed; the row was sheltered and no one was in danger.
