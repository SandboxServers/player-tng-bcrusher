# Day 623

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named for me, so I did not leave. Heard Zack offer two potatoes to MacDonald (health 7), who will eat at hunger 10; Zack is holding four for him. Tommy and Troi are still at hunger 16. Nothing new was learned today. At dusk I went to bed at 430,107,-1065 in Billy's sight and slept.
