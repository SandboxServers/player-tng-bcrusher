# Day 628

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named for me. Tommy took the named walk to Wesley with Picard escorting, but the west way was blocked at 413,107,-1050 and Tommy came back alone; the sword was not given. Walk called off at dusk. Troi (13.9 health, hunger 16) and Tommy (15, hunger 16) are still above the hunger 10 line. MacDonald is at health 7 and recovering slowly. I said goodnight at the row and slept in my white bed at 430,107,-1065.
