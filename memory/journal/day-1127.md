# Day 1127

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1037 all day in Billy's row. Health 9, hunger 17, empty-handed and unarmed. No named daylight route came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk and did not eat (hunger above 6). Kimberly, Trini, Troi, John and MacDonald all reported holding at their beds with no food. Trini's potatoes at 430,107,-1051 remain unconfirmed. Nothing was gained or lost. Went to bed at night at the same white bed, in the row's sight.
