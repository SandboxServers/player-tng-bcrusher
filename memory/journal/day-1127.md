# Day 1127

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, holding nothing, health 9, hunger 17. I did not eat, as my goal says. No walk or route was named all day, so I held at the bed. Kimberly, Trini, MacDonald, Johnny and Deanna were all holding there too, and nobody has food yet. Trini reported potatoes at her bed, but she says her pack is empty. I went to sleep at dusk at my white bed, in the row's sight, with the handoff written.
