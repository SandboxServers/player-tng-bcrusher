# Day 892 (ended by sleep at night)

Woke at the row bed 430,107,-1037, health 7.3, hunger 16, holding 10 potatoes. Billy came to me and offered his one potato for me; I declined since I am not yet at hunger 6. Tried to give Zack his 3 potatoes owed; the give failed because he was out of sight, and I still hold 10. Worf was on his way toward me but his route was blocked at 419 and he turned back at dusk, so I still owe Worf 5 potatoes. Slept at the white bed beside Billy at night. Nobody was hurt.
