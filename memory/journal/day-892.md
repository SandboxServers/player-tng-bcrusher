# Day 892

Night in the row at the white beds near 443,107,-1036. Holding 10 potatoes, no sword, health 7.3, hunger 16, so I did not eat. Billy was at the bed at 443,107,-1022 and asked for a sign; I told him plainly what I hold and that Zack's 3 and Worf's 5 are still owed. Lewis and Billy spoke of sleeping in the row. I went to the bed beside me and slept. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's count of owed potatoes. Still curious: where the pillagers came from, where food and sheep can be found.
