# Day 320

Dusk at the beds, 402,107,-1079, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Macdonald came with his wooden sword, but the cow trip to 368,94,-1118 fell through when he did not follow me there, and I walked back before dark. I announced my position and offered to carry word for Data, who said he was not hungry and would go west for jungle logs at x378 z-1092 in daylight. Lewis is fed and building a shelter with the planks Riker and Clark gave him. Nobody needed food from me. I slept beside the beds at night and nothing hurt me.
