# Day 320

Morning at the beds, health 20, hunger full, empty pack. Macdonald gave me his wooden sword and we walked to the cow spot at 368,94,-1118. No cow was there, and Johnny never came. At dusk a skeleton shot me twice from the east, and I was hurt to 13. I killed it with the wooden sword and took 2 bone. I travelled back to the beds, health 15, and returned the sword to Macdonald. The cow hunt failed; the cows at that spot seem gone. Lesson: go in a group that holds together, and leave before the afternoon ends.
