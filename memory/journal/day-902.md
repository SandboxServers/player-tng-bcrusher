# Day 902

Stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row all day. No one was in sight to give to, so Zack's 3 and Worf's 5 potatoes stayed owed. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. I told the row what I hold (10 potatoes, health 7.3, hunger 16). Troi, Lewis and MacDonald answered that they are holding at their beds too. Leonardo is holding for the night near 429,-1037 and will give Worf his potato at first light. Slept at my bed at dusk; nothing hurt me.
