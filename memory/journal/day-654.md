# Day 654

Held at my bed (430,107,-1065) all day in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk named by Billy; I did not walk. Trini was near me after a phantom attacked her at her bed; she is at health 17. MacDonald is at health 11, hunger 16, and Tommy at health 15, hunger 16; neither has reached hunger 10. I said good morning and good evening. Slept at my bed at night.
