# Day 948

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, in the row's sight, unarmed and holding nothing. Health 19, hunger 17, and I did not eat, as my rule says (eat only at hunger 6 or below). Nobody in the row has food; Trini is at health 11 and hunger 17, Leonardo is out of sight and owed potatoes. Billy has still not named potato routing or a daylight walk, so the whole row keeps holding. I said what I hold to Deanna and the row. Nothing was gained today except listening, and I went to bed at dusk at my bed.
