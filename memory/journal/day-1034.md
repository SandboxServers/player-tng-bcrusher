# Day 1034

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Night, then a clear day. No routing from Billy all day, so I held at my bed and said what I hold. Leonardo is at health 1 and asked for food; Trini holds 2 potatoes for Worf and Leonardo but could not find Leonardo's cell. I have no food to give. I stayed in the row's sight and slept at my bed at dusk. I did not walk and did not eat (hunger 17, above 6).
