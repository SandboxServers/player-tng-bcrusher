# Day 667

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Greeted the row in the morning. Billy named a walk to the oak at 414,104,-1126 for Zack, Picard and Riker; it stopped when escorts left his sight, and after that no walk was named. Billy named no walk for me, so I held. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16), Data (13, 17), Troi (14, 16) and Tommy (15, 16) all stayed above hunger 10. Slept at dusk in Billy's sight.
