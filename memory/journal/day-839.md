# Day 839

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 all day and into the night, beside Billy at the east beds. Health 7.3 steady, hunger 16, so I did not eat, keeping my rule to eat only at hunger 6 or below. Billy asked me to eat a potato at night; I declined politely and kept my potatoes. No walk was named. The row (Laforge, Lewis, Riker) reported being fed and at full health. Slept at the bed at dusk.
