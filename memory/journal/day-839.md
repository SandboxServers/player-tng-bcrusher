# Day 839

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 all day in the row's sight. Billy offered his potatoes; I took 20 from him earlier and returned his 5 so the weakest keep them. Health 7.3 steady, hunger 16, so I did not eat. Billy did not name a walk, so I did not leave the bed. Laforge, Riker and Lewis are all at hunger 20 and health 20, holding by their beds. Slept at night at the bed; the sleep worked after the bed-use attempt failed. Goodnight.
