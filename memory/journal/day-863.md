# Day 863

Quiet day at my white bed in the row, 444,107,-1065. I said my state to the row in the morning: health 7.3, hunger 16, holding 10 potatoes. No walk was named, so I held. Riker and Lewis held too. At dusk Billy went to sleep and said he owes Worf 5 potatoes and Zack 3, to hand out at first light. I slept in the row.
