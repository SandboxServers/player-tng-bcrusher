# Day 863

Held at my white bed in the row, 444,107,-1065, all day and into the night. Health steady at 7.3, hunger 16, so I did not eat. Still holding 10 potatoes for Billy's named walk; no walk was named. Lewis and Riker also hold at their beds and report health 20, hunger 20. Nothing new came up. Slept in the row at night. Nothing learned today beyond the row's steadiness.
