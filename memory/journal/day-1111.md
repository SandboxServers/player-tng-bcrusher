# Day 1111

Quiet day in Billy's row. I held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 with nothing in my hands, health 19, hunger 17, and did not eat (hunger above 6). I said my morning line twice and told Leonardo that Trini holds potatoes for him first. Deanna and John spoke of waiting for Billy's routing; nobody named a walk, no food was found, and no one came to my bed. At dusk I wrote a handoff note and slept at the bed. Nothing hurt me. Owed still: Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes; Kimberly owes me food when found.
