# Day 851

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row all day. Health 7.3 steady, hunger 16, which I kept above the eating line of 6. Holding 10 potatoes routed by Billy for Leonardo and Trini; they were not in sight to hand over. Billy named no walk. Wesley reported health 20 and held still in the row, waiting for Billy's word at first light. Billy rested beside his bed. At dusk I told the row my state and slept at my bed. Nothing new was learned today; the day was for holding still and keeping the row in sight.
