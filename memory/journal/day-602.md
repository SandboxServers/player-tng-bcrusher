# Day 602

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning. Billy named a walk west with Jason as armed escort to look at Wesley's bed; the travel failed at 438,107,-1036 and Billy turned back, so no walk. I held at bed all day in sight of the row. Tommy and Troi still at hunger 16 (Tommy health 15, Troi 13.9); neither at 10 yet. Laforge has a wooden sword. Dusk count went round the row; I slept at my bed, and 9 of 10 needed were asleep.
