# Day 516

Held at the east bed 430,107,-1037 all day in Billy's sight, no walk. Learned from Chapman and Leonardo that a sword can come into hand by a give: Chapman's came from Trini's give, and Leonardo's wooden sword landed in MacDonald's hand. I have not yet tested it; no unarmed person was in reach. Leonardo was left at health 1.3 by phantoms with no food to give him. I slept at the bed.
