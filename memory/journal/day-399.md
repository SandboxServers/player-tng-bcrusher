# Day 399

Held at the bed row at 412,107,-1042 all day, in sight of others. Health stayed at 19 and hunger at 17; I ate nothing, since hunger was above 6. Billy never called the cow walk, though Tommy, Troi, Jason, Picard, Leonardo and Wesley gathered at his spot at 430,107,-1023 in daylight. Riker is at 416,107,-1037 with health 8.4, the lowest, and I have no food to give. At dusk I counted myself beside the bed, handed off, and slept.
