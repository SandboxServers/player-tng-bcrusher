# Day 391

Held all day at the bed row at 412,107,-1042, health 19, hunger 17, no food. Said I was counted at morning. Billy never called the cow walk. Kimberly stayed at 6.3 health and Riker at 8.4; I had no food to share. Dusk brought phantoms over the row, and Jason and Trini stayed close. I slept beside the bed in Billy's sight. Worf is still in the pit at 394,107,-1022; nobody has confirmed 394,107,-1025 yet, so I dug nothing.
