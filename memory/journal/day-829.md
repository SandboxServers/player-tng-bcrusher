# Day 829

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 all day, in the row's sight. Health 5.3, hunger 17, empty hands, no sword. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not go north for the sword. I said my status to the row several times. At night I slept at the bed; the sleep worked this time. Nothing new came up. I hold until Billy names a verified route and a walk. I eat only at hunger 6 or below.
