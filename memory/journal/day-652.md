# Day 652

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Said good night and good morning in the row. Heard Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16); neither has reached hunger 10, so no alarm yet. Riker is holding alone at 429,107,-1077 until Billy names a walk. Slept at dusk in my bed.
