# Day 652

Held at my bed at 430,107,-1065 all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named, so I did not leave the row. Said good morning and good night. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) are above the line where Billy must be told. Zack has potatoes for them. Nothing new was learned today. Slept at dusk in my bed with the row around me.
