# Day 578

Held at my white bed at 444,107,-1065 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Billy named no walk for me. He fed Zack four potatoes and Tommy kept four. Many at the row asked for swords and beds; Billy named beds for Worf, Kimberly and himself. At dusk I slept in my bed in the row's sight.
