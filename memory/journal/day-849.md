# Day 849

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row all day. Told the row my state at morning: health 7.3, hunger 16, not eating. I hold 10 potatoes routed to me by Billy; he asked me to keep them for Leonardo and Trini and hand them over once they come into sight. They did not come into sight today. No walk was named, so I did not leave the row. Billy, Troi, Worf, Jason, Riker, MacDonald and Wesley all held at their beds. At dusk I slept at my bed; the row is sheltered and no one was in danger.
