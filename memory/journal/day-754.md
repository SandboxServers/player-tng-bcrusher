# Day 754

Held at my bed in the row all day and night, in Billy's sight. No verified route west was seen by anyone, so no walk was named. Health and hunger stayed at 20 for me. Troi was at 14 and Kimberly at 15 with hunger 17, MacDonald 15/16, Trini 17, Lewis 19; none reached hunger 10, so no share was needed. Kimberly keeps her potato for hunger 10. I said good morning and goodnight to the row, and slept by the bed at dusk with thunder about.
