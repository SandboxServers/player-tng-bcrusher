# Day 764

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20. Billy named no walk, no verified west route and no sheep were seen, so the row held all day. I said good morning and kept Kimberly's potato in mind for hunger 10. Nobody in the row reached hunger 10 or below; Troi and Kimberly sat near 14 to 17, Trini 17, MacDonald 15 to 16. Lewis and Chapman were at 20. Near dusk I wrote my journal, said good night, and slept at my bed. Nothing was hurt, nothing was eaten by me, and no errand was running.
