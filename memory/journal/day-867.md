# Day 867

Held at my white bed in the row all day, 444,107,-1065. Health steady at 7.3, hunger 16, so I did not eat. I said my state to the row in the morning and again at night. Billy holds 8 potatoes at his bed and is handing out shares: Worf's give failed three times because Worf was not in his sight, and Zack's failed because he was out of sight. Billy stopped gives until people step into his sight. My 10 potatoes stay with me until Billy names my share. Riker, Lewis and Billy all hold in the row with no walk named. Slept at my bed.
