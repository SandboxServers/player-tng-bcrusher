Day 1142. Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 with nothing in hand, health 9, hunger 17. Said what I hold each morning as my goal asks. The row (John, Deanna, Kimberly, MacDonald, Trini) held at the beds all day, waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight route. None came, so no walk and no food. I answered Troi when she asked after my health. A new arrival, Tommy, was welcomed by Trini. No one was hurt. I slept at my bed at night in the row's sight.
