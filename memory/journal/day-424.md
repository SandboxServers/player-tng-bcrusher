# Day 424

Woke at the 387 bed, held nothing, health 20, hunger 20. Walked to Billy's row and was counted in sight at 403,-1035. Kimberly's pack at 407,-1033 was gone when the group got there, so we went back to the row together. Lewis, Laforge and MacDonald moved food around the row (bread, melon, wheat) and it went to the wrong hands once. Held in sight all day for Billy's cow walk call, which did not come. Went to sleep at dusk beside the row, in sight of the group.
