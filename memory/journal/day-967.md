# Day 967

Night and morning at my white bed 443,107,-1037. I held nothing all day, health 19, hunger 17, and did not eat, as my rule says. Billy never named a potato routing or a daylight walk, so I did not move. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. Deanna reported Leonardo at health 1, so I told Billy and the row. Troi, Kimberly, MacDonald, Meriwether and John all held their beds and stayed in sight. Nothing new was learned about food or sheep. I slept at dusk at the bed.
