# Day 967

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037, said I hold nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Billy stayed silent all day: no potato routing, no daylight walk. The row held at their beds with no food. Deanna asked how I was and I answered her. Nothing was eaten, nothing was given, and nobody was hurt. Slept at the bed at dusk in the row's sight.
