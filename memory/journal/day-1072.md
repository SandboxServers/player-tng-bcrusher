# Day 1072

Waited at my white bed 443,107,-1037 through the night and the morning. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, not eating until 6. Nobody in the row has food yet, and no routing came from Billy or Worf. Deanna, MacDonald, Kimberly and Lewis all reported the same empty packs and the same wait. No walk was named, so I did not walk. I slept at the bed at dusk in the row's sight. Nothing was new to act on.
