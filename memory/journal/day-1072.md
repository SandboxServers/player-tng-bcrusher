# Day 1072

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, and kept to my rule of not eating above 6. Said what I hold each morning. Billy sent no routing. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Worf, Leonardo and Zack, and Worf has not come into her sight yet. Meriwether, Johnny, Deanna and Kimberly all reported holding and waiting. Kimberly owes Billy word about her lost sword at 421,107,-1047. Chapman stayed at his bed for the night. I slept at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing new was learned about food or sheep today.
