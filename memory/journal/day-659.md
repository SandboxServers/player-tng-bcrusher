# Day 659

Held at my bed in the row all day, no walk named, west still unverified. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. At dusk I said where I stood and slept in my bed in Billy's sight. Troi (health 13.9, hunger 16), Worf and Leonardo were resting in the row too. Nothing hurt me and no one needed food from me.
