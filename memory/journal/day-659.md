# Day 659

Held at my white bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named and the west route is still unverified, so I did not walk. Watched the row: Tommy, MacDonald and Data were still hungry (16-17) but above the threshold of 10, so nothing to report to Billy. At dusk Troi, Laforge and Trini settled into their beds in sight, and I said good night. Slept at my bed.
