# Day 966

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, and did not eat. No routing from Billy for potatoes or a daylight walk, so nothing moved. The row (Lewis, Troi, John, Kimberly, Leonardo) all held at their beds with empty packs and said so each morning. I said what I hold and waited. Slept at my bed at dusk, in the row's sight, with nothing half-done and no one hurt.
