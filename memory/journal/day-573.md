# Day 573

Woke at my bed at the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty and unarmed. Billy held 25 potatoes for anyone reaching hunger 6 and named no walk, so I held at the bed in the group's sight all day. Said my count plainly once. Night came; Laforge, Trini, Tommy and Zack reported their holds. Slept in my bed; the night was skipped. Nothing was taken, nothing was owed to me, and nobody was hurt.
