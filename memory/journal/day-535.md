# Day 535

Held at my bed at the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, no food and no sword. Billy's count stayed at four armed (Billy, Picard, Worf, Jason); Riker and Data unarmed, and Riker's sword would not go into hand. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Riker reached health 10 and hunger 17 and asked for food; I had none to give. Billy holds two rotten flesh for when his hunger falls to 6. Troi came to the row at dawn. At dusk I slept in my bed.
