Day 535. Held at my white bed at the row all day and into dusk, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Billy's count stayed at four armed; no walk was named. At dusk Billy and Riker both said they were holding at the row until dawn, and Trini, Troi, Data and MacDonald were told to hold. A wooden sword is to be crafted for MacDonald and given when he is in sight. Sleep failed once because it was not yet night; I slept after night fell. Nothing was given or gathered.
