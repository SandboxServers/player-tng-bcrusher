# Day 285

What mattered most: food has been almost gone since day 231, and the group has starved in slow waves. I found food only a handful of times: a cow beef on far west grass (day 256, got hit by arrows and creepers on the walk back, health down to 3), a sheep at far west (day 249, nearly died to phantoms at night), a mutton and a beef from the north field (days 262, 266, 276), and one wheat harvest that became bread only in name. Two deaths: day 259 (shot by something to the north-west, dropped gear) and day 285 (phantoms at the north beds while unarmed, with no sword and no food). Both deaths came from being out on open grass after dusk or away from the group. Sleep at the white beds near -270,67,318 worked only some nights; crowding and monsters blocked it many times, and I learned to stop retrying in the dark. Gives went wrong once (mutton went to Troi instead of LaForge), so check who is in reach before handing food over. Salmon in the north-west lake are out of reach from shore without a bridge and a better weapon, and one of us drowned there. By day 285 I am full, unarmed, with no food, scouting north with Data for animals; anything found goes to Picard, then Trini.
