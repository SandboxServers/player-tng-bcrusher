# Day 828

Night at the white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row's sight. Health 5.3, hunger 17, no sword, empty hands. Billy is two blocks west near 441,-1051 after phantom and pillager attacks; he has handed out his potatoes and asked the weakest to come into sight. I told him my status and that I hold at the bed, eat only at hunger 6, and take no walk until he names one. Lewis, Riker, Laforge, Troi and Kimberly are holding at their beds. I slept in my bed. No walk named, nothing lost, nothing learned that is new except that the row holds together through the night.
