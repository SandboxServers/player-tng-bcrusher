# Day 1144

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day in Billy's row. No route named, no food found, nothing in my hands. Health steady at 9, hunger 17, which is still above the eating line of 6. Told Troi I am steady when she asked, and she is watching my health. Kimberly, Troi, John and MacDonald all hold at their beds too; the row is waiting on Billy or Worf. Nothing new learned about food or sheep today. Slept at the bed in the row's sight.
