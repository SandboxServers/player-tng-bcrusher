Day 388 (the day that began at 387 and ran to dusk). Held at the bed row at 412,107,-1042 with Picard and Trini in sight all day. Health held at 19, hunger 17, no food held, so I did not eat. Riker and Kimberly were the lowest in the group and were fed by others. Billy never called the cow walk. Dug nothing at the pit. At dusk I slept beside the bed at 413,107,-1042; the sleep took with 9 of 10 asleep.
