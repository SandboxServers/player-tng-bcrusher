# Day 1079

Quiet day. I waited at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17 (not eating until 6). No routing from Billy and no walk named. The row (Trini, John, Kimberly, Meriwether, Troi) was also holding and waiting. I said what I hold and went to sleep at dusk in the row's sight. Nothing new learned about food or sheep. Next: tell Billy about hunger and health when they change; keep to the potatoes owed to Worf and Zack.
