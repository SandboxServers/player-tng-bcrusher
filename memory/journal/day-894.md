# Day 894

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row, health 7.3, hunger 16, holding 10 potatoes. Told the row what I hold. Billy never named a walk, so I did not walk. Leonardo went out in daylight to join Billy for Worf; Troi, Lewis and Chapman held at their beds. Zack and Worf were not in my sight, so nothing was given. Nothing hurt me. Slept at my bed at dusk, sheltered and out of danger.
