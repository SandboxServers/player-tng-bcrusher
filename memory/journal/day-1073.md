# Day 1073

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row's sight, holding nothing, hunger 17, health 19. Said what I hold and waited for Billy's routing all day. No routing came. Leonardo was reported at health 1 by Troi; I told Billy and offered what I have, which is nothing. Johnny, Meriwether, Deanna and Troi held at their beds too. Nobody walked, nobody ate, and I did not eat, since hunger stays above 6. Slept at the bed at dusk.
