# Day 1077

The row held all day at the white bed 443,107,-1037. No routing from Billy, no walk named, no food seen. Trini still holds 2 potatoes at 430,107,-1051 for Worf, Leonardo and Zack; Worf and Zack have not come into her sight on a checked cell. Meriwether reported health 13, hunger 17. Deanna, MacDonald, Kimberly, Troi and Lewis all said they hold with no food. I said what I hold (nothing) in the morning and waited. I slept at night at my bed, in the row's sight.
