# Day 660

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed, no food. In the morning Billy named a short daylight walk west to x=380 for himself, Picard and Clark, all armed. I was not named and not armed, so I stayed. Picard and Clark never reached Billy's view, so no walk went out and the row held at dusk. Others reported Worf holding with his sword, MacDonald at health 11 and hunger 16, Data at health 13 and hunger 17, Tommy at 15 and 16, Kimberly at 15 and 17. Nobody reached hunger 10. I slept at dusk in my bed; it went through.
