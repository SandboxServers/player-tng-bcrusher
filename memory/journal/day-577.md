# Day 577

Held at my white bed at 445,107,-1065 all day in the row's sight. Billy named no walk for me. At dusk Picard gave Tommy the wooden sword at Billy's request, and Billy brought Tommy and Zack in from the potato field. Heard the group settle into beds for the night. I have no food and no weapon, and I ate nothing. Slept at dusk. Nothing hurt me. Next: hold at bed until Billy names a walk.
