# Day 1089

Quiet day at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. I held nothing, health 19, hunger 17, and kept to the rule of not eating until 6. No routing from Billy came, and no walk was named, so I stayed put. Chapman and Troi spoke at dusk: Troi holds at bed 430 with no food to give. I said what I hold to the row, then slept at my bed in the row's sight. Still owed: Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes, which Trini holds at 430.
