# Day 1089
Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day. Said what I hold (nothing) in the morning. No routing from Billy and no walk named, so I did not walk. Health 19, hunger 17, not eating until 6. The row is holding: Lewis and Meriwether at health 13, Troi and Johnny with no food, Trini holding 2 potatoes at 430 for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Slept at the bed in the row's sight.
