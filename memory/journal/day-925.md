# Day 925

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day with the row. Said what I hold (nothing) and asked Billy for potato routing; he has not named a routing or a walk. Trini stayed beside me at health 11, holding two potatoes for Leonardo then Worf. Nobody walked, nobody was hurt, nobody gave food. Hunger stayed at 17, so I did not eat. Slept at my bed at night in the row's sight.
