# Day 833

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 all day, in sight of the row. Health steady at 7.3, hunger 16, empty hands, no sword. Billy never named a walk, so nobody went out. I told the row my state. Lewis, Riker and Laforge held at their beds with full health and hunger 20. I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. Slept at night in my bed.
