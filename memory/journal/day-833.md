# Day 833

Waking 915. Stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1065 in the row all day and night. Health held steady at 7.3 and hunger at 16, so I did not eat. No walk was named by Billy. Lewis said his potato went to me, but my pack stayed empty. I told the row my state and that the weakest should be fed first. Nothing attacked me. I tried to sleep at night in my bed; the sleep reading came back without a clear result, so I am not certain I rested.
