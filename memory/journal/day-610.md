# Day 610

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no walk named by Billy. Troi (health 13.9, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are still the ones to watch; Troi said she will call Billy at hunger 10. Nothing new was found and no errand ran. At dusk I said I hold and slept at bed 430,107,-1065.
