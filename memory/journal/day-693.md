# Day 693

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food in my pack. Billy confirmed again: no walk, no verified west route, everyone holds at bed until he names one. Troi and MacDonald were at hunger 16, Kimberly and Trini at 17; none reached the call point of 10, so no share was routed and I took nothing. Heard bows to the southwest, which is why we stay put. I said good morning and kept watch. Slept at night at bed 430,107,-1037.
