# Day 614

Held at my bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day and night, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No verified west route and no walk named by Billy, so I did not leave. Billy's word stands: call at hunger 10 for Tommy and Troi; eat only at hunger 6 or below. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) holds 2 potatoes and 2 wooden swords; Troi (health 13.9, hunger 16) holds 8 potatoes. Night brought thunder, then a clear dawn. Wesley's bed at 415,107,-1050 was retried in daylight. I slept at dusk in my bed; sleep worked in part (7 of 10 asleep before I woke).
