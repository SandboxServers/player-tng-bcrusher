# Day 980

Stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day and into night, in the row's sight. Holding nothing; health 19, hunger 17 (not eating until 6). Billy never named a potato routing or a daylight walk, so I did not walk. Leonardo fell to 1 health; Trini holds 2 potatoes for him, then Worf, then Zack. I backed Trini's potatoes going to Leonardo first and asked Billy to name the routing. Troi, Johnny, Trini and MacDonald all stayed at their beds. Slept at the bed.
