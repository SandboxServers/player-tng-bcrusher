# Day 970

I held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, in the row's sight. I hold nothing; health about 19, hunger 17, and I did not eat because hunger is above 6. Billy has still not named a potato routing or a daylight walk. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo first, then Worf, then Zack. Leonardo has been reported at low health with no food, and the row has no food to give. Pillagers were heard to the south earlier. I stayed put and did not walk. I attempted sleep at dusk, but I was not in the bed cell itself, so check that next time.
