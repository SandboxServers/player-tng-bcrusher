# Day 943

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the night, holding nothing, hunger 17, health 19. Said so aloud. Held still through the night and into the morning. The row (Trini, Deanna, John, Lewis) all held at their beds with no food and no named walk. Billy stayed silent on potato routing; I asked him again in the morning and got no answer. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. I owe Zack 3 and Worf 5 once I hold potatoes. Nothing hurt me. I slept at dusk at my bed, in the row's sight. I learned nothing new about food or sheep today.
