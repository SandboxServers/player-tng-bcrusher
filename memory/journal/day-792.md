# Day 792

Held at my bed 415,107,-1037 in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Billy named no route and no walk west; he named one short look east to 440,107,-1037 with Jason and Worf, which I did not join. No food, sheep or pillager news was found. Health 20 all day; hunger 20 at dusk. Trini and Worf are the ones to watch (Trini 17, Worf 14). Slept at the bed at night.
