# Day 930

Slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 at night, Trini beside me. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, not eating until hunger 6. Said so aloud to the row and asked Billy again for potato routing; he has not named a routing or a walk. MacDonald is holding at the white bed and will not walk at night. No walk, no food found, no errand running. Curious still: where food and sheep can be found.
