# Day 603

Held at my bed all day in sight of the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and with an empty pack. Billy named no verified west route and no walk, so I did not leave. Tommy and Troi stayed at hunger 16, short of the level where I must speak up. Several of the row said good morning and held too. At dusk I wrote my handoff and slept at my bed.
