# Day 1019

Slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, in the row's sight. Holding nothing; health 19, hunger 17 (did not eat; above 6). The row is walled in by planks on every side. Trini proposed breaking one west plank at 428,107,-1051 in daylight with a sword in sight; Troi, Lewis and MacDonald agreed only as Billy or Jason names and breaks it. Billy has named no routing and no walk, so I held at the bed all day. Nothing was done beyond speaking what I hold. Leonardo is still unseen. Worf was last reported at hunger 8.
