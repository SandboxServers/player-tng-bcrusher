# Day 413
Held all of day 413 at the bed row beside bed 401, at 402,107,-1037, in Billy's sight. Counted in at first light and said so. Billy's cow walk went out twice with five in sight and broke up both times, the group spreading 5 to 10 blocks; he called it off for another day, so I was never asked to join and never went. Hunger stayed at 17 and I held no food, so I did not eat (above 6). Health held at 17. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the group's sight and the sleep worked. Worf stayed alone in the pit at 394,107,-1022, not dug out.
