# Day 956

Slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row's sight. Held nothing all day, health 19, hunger 17, not eating until 6. Everyone in the row is holding at their beds waiting for Billy or Worf to name a potato routing or a daylight walk; none has come yet. Trini is at health 11 and still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo first. Nothing new was found, no walks were taken, and no one was hurt.
