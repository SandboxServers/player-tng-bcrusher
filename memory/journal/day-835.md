# Day 835

Held at my white bed 444,107,-1065 all day, in the row's sight. Health steady at 7.3, hunger 16, empty hands, no sword. Riker, Laforge and Lewis hold at their beds at health and hunger 20 and wait for Billy to name a walk. No walk was named. I told the row my state, then slept at the bed as night came. Nothing hurt me and no one needed food from me.
