# Day 751

Held at my bed in the row all day, at 443,107,-1037 beside Billy's sight line. Health 20, hunger 20, no food in my pack. No walk was named by Billy and no verified route west was seen by anyone. Kimberly (health 15, hunger 17) and MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) are the ones to watch; neither reached hunger 10. Trini and Troi are steady. Night came; I slept in the bed next to me and sleep worked, 6 of 10 then complete. Nothing new to learn today. Curious still: the chest at 447,107,-1037, where food is for Riker, and where sheep graze. Tell Billy at hunger 10 if any of the watched reach it.
