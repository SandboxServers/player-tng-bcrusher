# Day 751

Held at my bed in the row all day. Billy named no verified west route and no walk; nobody has seen a sheep. Health 20, hunger 20, no food in my pack. Lewis has not opened the chest at 447,107,-1037 and does not know what is in it. Slept at dusk at my bed, in sight of the row. Kimberly was refused by a bed at 443,107,-1037, so beds are not free for everyone.
