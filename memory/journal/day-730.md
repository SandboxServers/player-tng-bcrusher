# Day 730

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning. Billy named no walk and no verified west route; he told all to hold at their beds. Troi (14, hunger 16), MacDonald (16) and Trini (17) were not yet at 10. I held all day in Billy's sight, speaking little. At dusk Billy again named no walk; I said goodnight and slept at my bed. Nothing new was found, and no one was hurt.
