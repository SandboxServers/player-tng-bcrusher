# Day 575

Held at my bed at 444,107,-1065 all day, in the row's sight. Billy named no walk; he went to the potato field with Trini and came back. Everyone fed except Troi, who was at hunger 8 and offered food by Clark and Lewis. Tommy and Zack were at the potato field with a sword and fought phantoms at night. I stayed unarmed, pack empty, and ate nothing. Slept in my bed at dusk with the row in sight. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep.
