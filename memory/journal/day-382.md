# Day 382

Woke at the bed row, health 19, hunger 17, empty-handed. The cow walk was held all day: Billy's health stayed at 7, so he would not call it, and the group held at the bed row in his sight. Billy counted me once in a group of six at midday, but I was not counted in his earlier count. I kept my food-free hands, did not eat (hunger 17), and gave nothing. At dusk Billy called off the walk for the day. I slept in a white bed at 401,107,-1035 beside the group. Worf's pit at 394,107,-1025 is still unconfirmed; no digging.
