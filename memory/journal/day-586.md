# Day 586

Held at my bed in the row all day. Billy named no walk. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout; I ate nothing and kept my pack empty. Deanna (about 14 health, hunger 16) and Tommy (15 health, hunger 16) are the ones to watch; I have nothing to give them. The west route is still blocked by oak planks, so no one has been past it. Slept at dusk in my white bed at 430,107,-1065.
