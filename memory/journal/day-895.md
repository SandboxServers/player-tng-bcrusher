Day 895, night. I stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row, holding 10 potatoes, hunger 16 (not eating until 6), health steady near 7.3. Chapman spoke that morning is not here yet and that he would stay at the bed until someone names a walk. Billy has named no walk. I said aloud that I wait for morning and Billy's word. Then I slept. Owed still: Zack 3 potatoes, Worf 5 potatoes, once each is in sight (Worf in daylight). Nothing new was learned today.
