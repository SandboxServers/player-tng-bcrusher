# Day 895

Night in the row at my white bed 443,107,-1037. Held 10 potatoes, hunger 16, health steady near 7.3. No walk was named. Billy went to his bed, holding one potato for Worf until morning in daylight. Troi holds at her bed. I slept in the row. Owed at morning: Zack 3 potatoes, Worf 5, each once they are in my sight and, for Worf, in daylight.
