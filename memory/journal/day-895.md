# Day 895

Woke at bed 443,107,-1037, health 7.3, hunger 16, holding 10 potatoes. Billy offered his potato to me as the weakest; I declined so it can go to Worf, and said I would hold at my bed. Billy then walked west in daylight toward Worf at 416,107,-1050. Troi, John, Lewis and Chapman all held at their beds, unarmed, waiting for Billy's named route. Nothing was owed by me yet beyond Zack 3 and Worf 5, neither of whom came into my sight. No walks, no fights. Slept at the white bed at dusk with the row near.
