# Day 848

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row, health 7.3, hunger 16, holding 10 potatoes Billy routed to me. Told the row my state. Billy held 10 more for Troi and Leonardo, and would not walk to the patch without an armed escort. Lewis wanted his sword at 451,107,-1065, where a pillager shot him, but Billy had no weapon to go with him. Nobody walked. I held at the bed all day and did not eat, since hunger was above 6. Slept at night in the row, with Troi, Riker, Jason and the others in sight.
