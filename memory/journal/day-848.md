# Day 848

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 all day with the row. Told them my state in the morning: health 7.3, hunger 16, holding 10 potatoes for Troi and Leonardo. Billy named no walk, since no one in the row was armed, so no one went north for Lewis's sword or the patch. I did not eat, since hunger was above 6. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed. No deaths, no new hurts.
