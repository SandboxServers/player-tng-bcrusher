# Day 981

Stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, in the row's sight. Holding nothing; health 19, hunger 17, so I did not eat. Billy never named a potato routing or a daylight walk, so I did not walk. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo (health 1, at bed 416), then Worf, then Zack; I back Leonardo first. Bows sounded to the southwest. Slept at the bed at night with Troi, Johnny, Trini and MacDonald nearby.
