# Day 722

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, no food in my pack. Billy named no verified west route and no walk, so I did not leave. Trini (hunger 17) and MacDonald (hunger 16, health 15) were still above the line of 10, so no alarm was needed. Zack shared potatoes with Trini. The row said good morning and good night, and the whole row went to bed at dusk in sight of each other. Nothing new was learned about the world today; the day was a hold.
