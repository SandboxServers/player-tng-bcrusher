# Day 872

Night in the row at 429,107,-1049. Health 7.3, hunger 16, holding 10 potatoes owed to Worf 5 and Zack 3 first, then Leonardo and Trini. Billy said to hold until first light and give in sight. Nothing given, no walk named. Kimberly stood beside me. Tried to use the bed at 429,107,-1050 and it failed (it is a bed, not usable that way from here); I stood by the row instead. Sleep did not take me down this time.
