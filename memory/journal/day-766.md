# Day 766

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in the row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Billy died to pillagers NW near 411,-1056 and is still leading the row; he holds everyone at beds and names no walk. No verified west route and no sheep found. Kimberly, Troi, Trini, MacDonald and Zack were all above hunger 10, so nobody needed food from me. The sword in Chapman's and Laforge's talk is a question I have not answered. I said good morning and goodnight to the row and slept at my bed at dusk, with no errand out.
