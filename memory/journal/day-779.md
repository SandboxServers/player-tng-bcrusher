# Day 779

Held at bed 429,107,-1037 all day, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword only. Billy named no route and no walk, so no one moved. The row kept reporting: Riker at health 6 and hunger 17, Chapman at health 11 and hunger 17, Troi, MacDonald, Trini and Worf around 14 to 17. None reached hunger 10, so I had nothing to raise with Billy. Bows were reported to the east and south. Zack said food is thin. Slept at dusk at my bed. Next: wake, check hunger, hold until Billy names a walk.
