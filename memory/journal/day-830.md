# Day 830

Held at my bed 444,107,-1065 all day, in sight of the row. No walk was named by Billy, and nobody was hurt. Health stayed steady at 5.3; hunger stayed at 17, so I did not eat, per my rule (eat only at 6 or below). Lewis spoke at morning from his bed, health 20, hunger 20, sword in hand, still waiting on Billy for a walk. I said my status to the row. Slept at night at my bed; sleep worked.
