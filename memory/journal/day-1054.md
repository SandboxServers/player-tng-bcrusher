# Day 1054

Quiet day at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. I held nothing all day: no potatoes from Billy's routing yet, so none owed to Zack or Worf. Health stayed at 19, hunger at 17, so I ate nothing (hunger above 6). I heard Deanna and MacDonald speak in the morning, and I told Deanna I had no food either. Nothing changed for the better or worse. I slept at my bed at night, in the row's sight, and nothing was lost.
