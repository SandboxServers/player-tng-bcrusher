# Day 921

Stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day and into dusk, in the row's sight. Holding nothing, hunger 17, health steady at 19.3. Said what I hold to the row and to Trini. Trini keeps her two potatoes for Leonardo first, then Worf. Billy has still named no potato routing and no walk, so nobody walked. Lewis tried another bed at 443,-1050 because the nearer ones refused. Thunder came in the afternoon. Slept at dusk at my bed.
