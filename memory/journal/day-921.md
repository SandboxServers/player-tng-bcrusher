# Day 921

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the dark, holding nothing, health 19.3, hunger 17. Trini was here holding one potato, owed to Leonardo first. No food routing or walk was named by Billy. The signs at the beds mark Kimberly and MacDonald as shot on day 1061. I said what I hold, wrote to my journal, and slept at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing has been eaten; hunger stays above 6, so I wait. Next waking: say what I hold, ask Billy for routing, no walk without his named call in daylight.
