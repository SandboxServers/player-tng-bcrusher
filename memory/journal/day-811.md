# Day 811

Held at my bed in the row all day, as Billy has named no walk. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword only. Sleep was refused in daylight twice, then worked at night. The row is holding: Worf at hunger 13, Trini at health 12 with three potatoes for the weakest, Data watching an enderman to the southeast, Jason, Zack, John and MacDonald all waiting on Billy's word. Nothing hurt me and I took nothing I am not owed.
