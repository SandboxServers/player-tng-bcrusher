# Day 752

Held at the row all day, health 20, hunger 20. No walk named by Billy, no verified west route. At night Billy, Picard and Lewis were near the beds. My bed use failed (a bed is not used that way), so I stood beside the bed at 443,107,-1065 and slept; the night was skipped. Nothing new to act on. Still curious about the chests at 447,107,-1037 and 433,107,-1065, food for Riker, and where sheep graze.
