# Day 752

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20. No walk named by Billy, no verified west route. Picard and Trini were in reach at dusk. Chapman, Lewis and Kimberly are holding in the row too; Kimberly is at health 15, hunger 17, and keeps a potato for 10. I slept at dusk by the bed at 443,107,-1051 and the sleep worked for 7 of 10 needed. Nothing new to learn today; I will watch Kimberly and Troi and tell Billy when anyone reaches hunger 10.
