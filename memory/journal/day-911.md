# Day 911

Dusk at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row. Holding nothing: no food, no sword. Health 19.3, hunger 17, so I did not eat. Trini is near with her two potatoes; she is well (health 15) and will not walk in the dark. Macdonald holds at his bed, unarmed. Nothing new was learned. Night came, and I slept in my bed.
