# Day 926

Night at my white bed 443,107,-1037 with Trini beside me. Holding nothing, health 19.3, hunger 17, so I did not eat. Heard Troi hold at his bed through the night and say he will ask after everyone at first light. I said aloud where I was and what I hold, then slept. Billy has still named no routing or walk, so I stay put until morning and do not walk in the dark.
