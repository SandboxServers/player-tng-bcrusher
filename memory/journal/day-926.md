# Day 926

Waking at my white bed 443,107,-1037. Holding nothing, health 19.3, hunger 17, not eating until 6. The row is holding at the beds with no food. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo first, then Worf. Billy has not named a routing or a walk. Troi, John and MacDonald each said they hold until a daylight route is named. I asked Billy for potato routing and got no answer. No walk was called. Slept at dusk in the row's sight, thunder overhead, not in danger.
