# Day 926

Dusk came with thunder at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. I hold nothing, health 19.3, hunger 17, and I did not eat. I said what I hold and asked Billy for potato routing; he has named no routing and no walk yet. Trini stays by me with her 2 potatoes for Leonardo first. Kimberly, John, Troi and Chapman hold at their beds and will not walk in the dark. Overheard: two more deaths by shooting on day 1061 near the row's signs. I slept in my bed in the row's sight.
