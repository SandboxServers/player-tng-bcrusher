# Day 926

Quiet night at my white bed 443,107,-1037, with Trini beside me in the row's sight. Health 19.3, hunger 17, holding nothing. Troi greeted me from his bed and said he would hold through the night. I told him where I stood. No walk, no eating until hunger 6, and no sword. Slept through the storm. Still waiting on Billy for potato routing; I still owe Zack 3 and Worf 5 once I hold them.
