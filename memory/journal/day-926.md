# Day 926

Night at my white bed 443,107,-1037, Trini beside me, in the row's sight. Holding nothing, health 19.3, hunger 17, not eating until 6. I said what I hold aloud and asked Billy for potato routing; no answer yet. MacDonald spoke from the bed at 430,107,-1023: holding through the night, no walk. Two signs in the row name fallen people from day 1061, so the west is still dangerous. No walk, no move. I slept. Nothing new to act on until morning.
