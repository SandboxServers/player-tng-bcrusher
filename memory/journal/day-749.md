# Day 749

Held at my bed in the row all day, as before. No walk was named by Billy and no verified west route came from anyone. Trini, Kimberly and Picard were in sight of the row at evening. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword. I said good evening to the row, wrote my journal, and slept in my bed at night. Kimberly reported she went to her bed as Billy named, and Trini went to hers in sight of the row.
