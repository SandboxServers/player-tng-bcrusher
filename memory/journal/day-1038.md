# Day 1038

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Said what I hold. No routing came from Billy all day; the row held at its beds in daylight and no one walked. Trini still holds two potatoes for Worf and Leonardo, and waits for a named cell. Zack reported health 13, hunger 14, no food; Lewis health 13, hunger 17. I ate nothing and walked nowhere. Slept at my bed at night, sealed in the row's sight. Nothing hurt me.
