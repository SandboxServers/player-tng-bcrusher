# Day 622

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named for me, so I stayed put. Heard that Picard is ready to escort Tommy to Wesley once Billy calls it, and that Tommy and Troi are still at health 15 and 14, hunger 16. MacDonald is at health 7, so I offered him help. Slept at dusk in my bed at 430,107,-1065.
