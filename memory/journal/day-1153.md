# Day 1153

Dusk and night at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Kimberly was in reach. I said what I hold: nothing, unarmed, health 9, hunger 17. No food found, no walk named, nothing taken. Trini, MacDonald and Troi held at their beds through the night, and bows were drawn to the southwest. I slept at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing new learned about food or sheep.
