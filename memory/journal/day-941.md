# Day 941

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, at night. Holding nothing, hunger 17, health steady at 19.3. Did not eat; hunger is above 6. Said where I stand aloud. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo first, and is armed at the row. MacDonald holds at white bed 430,107,-1023, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No food found yet, and no sheep. No walk was named by Billy. Slept at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing new learned about the world today beyond the row itself.
