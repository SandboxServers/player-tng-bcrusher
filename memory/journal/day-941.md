# Day 941

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 holding nothing, hunger 17, health 19. Said what I hold and asked Billy for potato routing; he has not named one, and no daylight walk was called. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo first. John, Troi and Trini all held at their beds waiting on Billy. Nobody moved. I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. Nothing hurt me. Dusk came and I slept in my bed in the row's sight. Still unknown: where food is and where sheep are.
