# Day 776

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, no food. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Riker fell to health 6 and hunger 16; Lewis gave him one potato, and I said plainly that I hold no food. Chapman is at health 11, hunger 17. Wesley and Zack hold in the row. No sheep, west route or pillager news came from anyone. Slept at dusk in my bed at 429,107,-1037 with the group near.
