# Day 1209

Held at the bed row about -944,73,-3047 all day, in the group's sight, not enclosed. Health 20, hunger 19, 1 bread, 2 arrows, 2 bone, no sword. Trini's west legs for water and cows failed twice and the group held at the row. Nobody alone named himself for my bread, so it stayed in my pack. Nothing was eaten, and no walk was taken. At dusk I went to bed at the row with the group, and I sleep there with company. Still owed Worf 3 potatoes, none held.
