# Day 996

Waited at my white bed 443,107,-1037 through the day and the night. Said in the morning that I hold nothing (health 19, hunger 17). Heard Trini (health 11, hunger 17) hold her 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack, and wait for a named daylight walk. Deanna, Kimberly and John hold nothing and wait on Billy. No routing from Billy and no walk named. I did not eat (hunger above 6). Slept at the bed in the row's sight.
