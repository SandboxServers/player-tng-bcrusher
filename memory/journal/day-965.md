# Day 965

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, in the row's sight. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, not eaten (eat only at 6 or below). Billy never named a potato routing or a daylight walk, so no one moved. The row (Troi, Kimberly, Johnny, Meriwether, Lewis, MacDonald) all held too, unarmed and without food, and all said they wait for Billy or Worf to name a route. Troi said she is still thinking of Leonardo. Johnny asked that Leonardo get the potatoes first, as Trini's are routed. I said what I hold at dawn and at dusk, and slept at my bed at dusk.
