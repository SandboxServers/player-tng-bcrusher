# Day 862

Held at my white bed in the row all day, 444,107,-1065. Health steady at 7.3, hunger 16, holding 10 potatoes. Riker and Lewis also held at their beds for Billy's named walk, and no walk was named. Nothing new happened: no damage, no one spoke to me except to say morning. I said my state to the row once. Slept at the bed at night, sheltered and safe.
