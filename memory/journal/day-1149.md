# Day 1149

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day in Billy's row, holding nothing and unarmed. Health 9 and hunger 17 the whole day, so I did not eat (hunger above 6). No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, and no food turned up. The row spoke of potatoes at Trini's bed 430,107,-1051, but Trini has none now. Troi, Kimberly, John, MacDonald and Trini all held at their beds and are waiting for the same routing. I said my state aloud when Troi asked, and slept at my bed at dusk with the row near.
