# Day 1149

Slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. No daylight route was named, so nobody walked and I did not leave the bed. I said what I hold (nothing, unarmed) and answered Troi's question about my health: 9, hunger 17, steady. Trini has potatoes at 430,107,-1051; I owe Zack 3 and Worf 5 once I hold any. Nothing new found today.
