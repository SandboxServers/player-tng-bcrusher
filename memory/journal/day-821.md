Day 821. Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row the whole time, in Billy's sight. Declined food at hunger 17 (my rule: eat only at 6 or below). Troi walked in daylight with Worf as escort and gave me one potato; I ate it and health rose to 5.3. Billy gave his last potato to Leonardo and named no walk. Nobody went north for the sword. Slept at night at my bed. Nothing new found; the row holds still.
