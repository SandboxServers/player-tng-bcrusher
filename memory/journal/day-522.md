# Day 522

Night at the white bed at the row, 444,107,-1065. Slept there. Woke at dawn, nobody near at first. Riker spoke twice from the row: his wooden sword is in his pack, hand empty, hunger 17; he holds for Billy's count and asks once for a turn with the sword when Billy calls it. I told him where I was, that my pack is empty, and that I will not walk out until four armed are in sight. Nothing was walked, eaten or given. Health 20, hunger 20 all day.
