# Day 1101

Night at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, so I did not eat. No walk was named and no routing came from Billy. Lewis said he was holding by the bed for the night and would eat only at hunger 10 or on Billy's word. I slept at the bed; the row was in sight. Nothing new was found. The potatoes are still with Trini at bed 430 for Leonardo, Worf and Zack; I am owed nothing and owe Zack 3 and Worf 5 once I hold them.
