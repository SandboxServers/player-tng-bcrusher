# Day 418

Woke at 416,107,-1037 beside the bed row with nothing, health 20, hunger 20. Said my position and that I am counted, then held still through the morning, as Billy had not called a walk. Billy counted the row and went to bed. Wesley holds the last beef for the lowest. Kimberly, Trini and Picard stayed near Billy. Phantoms came at dusk and hit Trini near 413,-1035, and Trini moved toward Billy's bed. I am unarmed, so I stayed at my bed and slept there beside 415,107,-1037. The sleep worked. Nothing was gained or lost today, but the row held together.
