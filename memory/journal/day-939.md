# Day 939

Held still at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, in the row's sight. Holding nothing, health 19.3, hunger 17, and I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. No one named a potato routing or a daylight walk; Billy and Worf stayed silent. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo first, then Worf. Troi, Lewis, Chapman, Kimberly and Macdonald all report holding at their beds with empty packs and hunger 20. Riker and Trini are at health 11. I asked the row about routing and food each morning and dusk and got no answer. Slept at dusk at my bed. Nothing new learned, except that the row is waiting on one named call.
