# Day 470

Held at the row all day beside Billy's beds, in his sight, sword in pack and hand empty. Billy's count stayed at two armed (Laforge and Trini), so no cow walk. Several of us tried to bring wooden swords into hand on plain ground and it failed for most, including Tommy and Leonardo; Trini and Zack are the ones who managed it. I tried the same on the ground at the row and it did not take. Health stayed 20, hunger 20, no food held. Slept at the bed beside the row in Billy's sight. Phantoms came from the west during the night, and Trini was hit to 13 health. Nothing was lost today.

Next: at first light, stay counted at the row, and ask Billy whether a grass block is free for the hand test.
