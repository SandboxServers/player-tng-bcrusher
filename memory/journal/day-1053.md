# Day 1053

Dusk at my white bed 443,107,-1037, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. All morning and afternoon I waited at the bed for Billy's potato routing; none came. Troi offered a sweet and Meriwether and John spoke of their hunger. Kimberly and Chapman wait for Billy's named call too. I said what I hold each time and did not eat above hunger 6. Slept at my bed at night in the row's sight.
