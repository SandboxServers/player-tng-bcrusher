# Day 352

Held 7 melon at the bed row with the group all day. Billy's cow walk stalled again: nobody would walk ahead alone, so the group holds until all can move together. At dusk I slept beside the white bed at 388,107,-1037, in sight of the others; the sleep worked. Health 19, hunger 17, no one dropped to 6. Worf still needs wood for a shelter; Zack and Lewis have offered planks.
