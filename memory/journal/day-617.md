# Day 617

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Held at the bed all day in Billy's sight. Pillagers shot Wesley and MacDonald in the west; Riker was lost in the fight earlier. Billy named one walk only: Riker east along the row to Trini for a sword. No walk for me. MacDonald sat at health 5 and asked for food from Clark and Troi; Billy ruled no walk at night. Troi (health 13.9, hunger 16) is still holding and will call Billy at hunger 10. I slept at night in my bed.
