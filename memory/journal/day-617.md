# Day 617

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Billy named no walk for me; he named only Riker's sword and a walk for Riker to Trini. Pillagers shot Wesley and MacDonald in the west. Troi (health 14, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are above the hunger-10 line. Worf, Picard, Jason, Laforge and Billy all went to bed in sight at dusk. I slept at my bed at night.
