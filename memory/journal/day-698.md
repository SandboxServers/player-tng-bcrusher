# Day 698

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword, no food. Said good morning to the row. Billy named no walk and no verified west route, and bows were heard southwest, so the row held all day in sight. Nobody reached hunger 10; MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Troi (health 14, hunger 16) were the most worn, and Kimberly, Trini and Lewis were near 17. The far beds at 443,107,-1022 and -1051 refused some of the row, which Billy and Kimberly noticed at dusk. I slept at dusk at my bed, Billy's sight.
