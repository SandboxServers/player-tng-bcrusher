# Day 955

Slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row's sight. Health 19, hunger 17, holding nothing, no death. Nobody spoke to me directly; the row is out of food. Leonardo is at health 1 and Trini holds 2 potatoes for him. Billy has not named a potato routing or a daylight walk, so I did not walk. I said what I hold at morning and waited at the bed. I did not eat, since hunger is above 6.
