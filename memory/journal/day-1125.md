# Day 1125

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Nothing to eat, nothing to carry, no route named by Billy or Worf. I said what I hold at dawn. Kimberly, Troi, MacDonald, Trini and Johnny all report the same: empty-handed, waiting for a named daylight call. At dusk I walked the one block to the bed and slept; the sleep took, with 3 of 10 needed asleep. Health 9, hunger 17, not eating until 6.
