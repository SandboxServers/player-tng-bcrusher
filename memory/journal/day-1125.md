# Day 1125

Dusk at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Holding nothing, health 9, hunger 17, so I did not eat. No walks were named and no food was found. Kimberly stood in reach and MacDonald was resting at his bed nearby. I said what I hold and went to sleep in the row's sight. Owed still: Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes.
