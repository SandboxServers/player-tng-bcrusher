# Day 735

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 all day, health 20, hunger 20. The row held too: nobody reached hunger 10 and no verified route west was named, so no walk. Billy kept asking for a verified bed square and route. MacDonald, Troi, Kimberly and Trini are all still hungry and waiting. I said plainly that I hold no route. Slept at night in my bed, in Billy's sight.
