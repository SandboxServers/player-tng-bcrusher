# Day 735

Held in the row all day at my bed with Billy's group in sight. No verified route west was named, so no walk. Everyone stayed at 20 or 19 hunger, except Troi (17), Trini, Kimberly and MacDonald (16 to 17), none at 10, so no food was passed out. Bows were drawn to the southwest and west several times during the night; I stayed in the row. I slept beside the row at night and went back to sleep after a partial rest. Nothing new learned about the west.
