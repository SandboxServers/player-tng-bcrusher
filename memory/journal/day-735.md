# Day 735

Held at bed 443,107,-1037 in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody reached hunger 10 overnight, though Troi was at health 13.9 and hunger 17, and MacDonald at health 15, hunger 16. Billy and Picard both report no verified route west and no walk named, so no walk. Bows were heard drawn to the southwest and west all day and night, never near us. Billy is looking at the beds for a verified square; a request from Wesley about -1051 is pending his answer. Slept at night in the row, 8 of 10 needed asleep.
