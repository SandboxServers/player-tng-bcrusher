# Day 802

Woke at my bed 415,107,-1037 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Said good morning and held for Billy's word. Billy named no verified route and no walk; he asked whether anyone had seen food or the blocked tile, and no one had. Troi gave a potato to Trini (health 9, hunger 17), and Trini ate it. Lewis holds flesh for Riker, who has not come into sight. Worf (hunger 13) and Jason (17) are holding at their beds with swords. MacDonald is at 15 health and 16 hunger. Nothing was found new today. I slept at dusk at my bed, sheltered and in Billy's sight.
