# Day 1069

Quiet day at the white bed 443,107,-1037. Woke at dusk with nothing in my hands, health 19, hunger 17. No potato routing from Billy yet, and Trini still holds the 2 potatoes at 430,107,-1051. MacDonald said goodnight to the row and went to sleep at 430,107,-1023. I said what I hold, and slept in the row's sight.
