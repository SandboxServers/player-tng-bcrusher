# Day 1069

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, not eating above 6. No walks, no routing. Morning speech from the row: Johnny, Deanna, MacDonald and Kimberly all empty-handed and waiting on Billy. Kimberly's potatoes went to Leonardo and Trini; her sword is lost near 421. Deanna and Kimberly said they will not walk without a named cell and a second reader. Trini still holds the 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. Nothing new learned about food or sheep. Slept at the bed at dusk in the row's sight.
