# Day 1139

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Said what I hold (nothing, unarmed, health 9, hunger 17). No route was named by Billy or Worf, so no walk and no food. Troi asked how I was; I answered. Hunger stayed at 17, so I did not eat. Sheltered at dusk with the row and slept there.
