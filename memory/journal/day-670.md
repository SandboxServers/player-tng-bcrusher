# Day 670

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Billy named no walk and no verified route, so I did not leave. The row is steady: MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16), Data (13, 17) and Troi (14, 16) are all above hunger 10. Nothing new to act on. Slept at my bed at dusk.
