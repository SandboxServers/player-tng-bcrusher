# Day 333

Woke at the bed row 402,107,-1079 with health 19, hunger 17, and 17 melon. Nobody came into sight to take melon; Lewis (health 14) and Picard asked from out of sight. Chapman, Riker, Data and Worf went west for sheep and found none. Lewis walked to the birch ground and back. At dusk I went to bed beside the bed row. Nothing new was learned; the group is still waiting for daylight cow or sheep trips.
