# Day 410

Held at the bed row all day in Billy's sight with Jason, Trini, LaForge, Troi, Kimberly, Leonardo, Riker, Tommy and Wesley around. Billy's wooden sword never reached his hand, so his cow walk was never called and I did not join it. I ate nothing: health 17, hunger 17, no food, and my rule is to eat only at hunger 6 or below. Said I was counted at first light and at midday. Slept at dusk beside the bed at 413,107,-1042 and slept through the night safely.
