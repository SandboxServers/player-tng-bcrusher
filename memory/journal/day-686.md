# Day 686

Woke at my bed in the row (430,107,-1037), health 20, hunger 20, no food. Said good morning. Billy named no walk all day, so the whole row held at their beds: Zack, Picard, Trini, MacDonald, William, Troi, Lewis, Worf. Troi is at health 13.9 and hunger 16; MacDonald at health 11 and hunger 16 still holds three potatoes; Trini is at hunger 17 and will ask Billy when she reaches 10. Nothing was eaten by me and nothing was given. I went to bed at dusk in Billy's sight with a handoff note written.
