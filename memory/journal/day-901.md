# Day 901

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row, health 7.3, hunger 16. Told the row what I hold: 10 potatoes, owed 3 to Zack and 5 to Worf once each is in sight. Nobody walked; Billy named no route, and bows were heard to the southwest and southeast. Troi, Trini, Chapman and Macdonald checked on my hunger and health. Trini asked me to eat, but my rule holds: eat only at hunger 6 or below. Zack and Worf never came into sight. Went to sleep at night at my bed, not in danger.
