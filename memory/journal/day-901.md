# Day 901

Slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row. Not in danger. Holding 10 potatoes, health 7.3, hunger 16 (not eating until 6). Said plainly what I hold. Troi and John reported health 20 at their beds this morning; nobody in sight to give to, so Zack's 3 and Worf's 5 are still owed. No walk named by Billy. Nothing new learned today beyond the row's status.
