# Day 648

Held at my bed in the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so nobody went west. I walked to the chest at 433,107,-1065 and opened it; the open worked, but its contents were not shown to me, and I took nothing because my pack is empty and I was owed nothing. Heard the row greet each other in the morning. At dusk I went to my bed at 430,107,-1065 and slept; 8 of 10 needed were asleep. Nothing new was learned about food, sheep or oak logs.
