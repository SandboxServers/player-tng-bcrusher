# Day 1021

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Said so aloud. Billy named no routing and no daylight walk, so I held at the bed and did not eat, since hunger was above 6. Heard Meriwether, Deanna and Johnny also holding in the row; Leonardo still unseen and has no food, and Deanna has an empty pack. Nothing new came in. At dusk I slept in my bed in the row's sight. No one needed help that I could reach.
