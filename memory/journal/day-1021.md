# Day 1021

The row stayed walled in by planks all day. No walk was named by Billy or Jason, and no potato routing came. I held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 with nothing in my hands, health 19, hunger 17, and did not eat because hunger was above 6. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo (health 1, unseen), then Zack and Worf. Troi, Lewis, MacDonald, Chapman and Kimberly all held at their beds too, and no one broke out. I slept at my bed in the row's sight. Next waking: say what I hold, then wait at the bed for Billy's routing.
