# Day 1046

Stood at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Said what I hold on waking. No routing came from Billy; Trini still holds the 2 potatoes at 430,107,-1051, meant for Leonardo first, then Worf, then Zack. Deanna, John and Meriwether all report waiting on a named call. Nothing was done beyond waiting and speaking. Slept at my bed in the row's sight.
