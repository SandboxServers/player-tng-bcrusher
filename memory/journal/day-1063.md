Day 1063. Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Said my state aloud and held at the bed all day for Billy's potato routing, which never came. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo then Worf. Kimberly, Meriwether, John and Troi are all in the row, all holding, all waiting for a named call. Nobody walked. Did not eat (hunger above 6). Slept at the bed at dusk in the row's sight.
