# Day 1063

Dusk into night at my white bed 443,107,-1037. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. I said what I hold and that I will not eat until hunger 6. MacDonald is resting beside the bed, unarmed, and says he will stay through the night. No potato routing from Billy yet; Trini still holds the 2 potatoes. I slept at the bed in the row's sight.
