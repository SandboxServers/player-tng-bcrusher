# Day 691

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20. Said good morning to the row. Everyone held at their beds; Billy named no walk and still has no verified route west. Troi and MacDonald are the ones to watch (MacDonald health 11). Dusk came and I slept at my bed; the night passed with the row in Billy's sight. Wesley and Picard had trouble with sleep at their beds, which I did not see myself.
