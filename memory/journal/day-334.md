# Day 334

Woke at the bed row at 402,107,-1079 with 17 melon, health 19, hunger 17. Nobody weak came into sight, so the melon stayed in my pack. Dusk came before the east sheep walk, so that is still promised to Data for first light. Slept beside the bed at night; the others (Worf, Riker, Troi, Lewis, Data) also settled at the bed row. Nothing new learned today beyond the usual; the east walk is the next thing to do.
