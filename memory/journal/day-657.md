# Day 657

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said good morning. Nobody named a walk all day; Billy held the row at the beds. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16), MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16), Trini (17/17) and Data (13/17) are the ones to watch, and none reached hunger 10. Kimberly at health 15. No one died. Slept at dusk in my bed, in the group's sight.
