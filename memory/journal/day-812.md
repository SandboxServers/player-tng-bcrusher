# Day 812

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so I stayed put. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout; I hold no food. John is at health 11 and hunger 17, and Trini offered him a potato. Worf is at hunger 13 and will call for food at 10; Zack holds two potatoes for him, and MacDonald holds three. I had nothing to share and said so by saying good morning. Slept at dusk at 415,107,-1037.
