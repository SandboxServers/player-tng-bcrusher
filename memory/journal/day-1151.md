# Day 1151

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1037 all day with the row. Health 9, hunger 17, holding nothing, unarmed. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, so nobody walked. Troi, Kimberly, MacDonald, John and Trini all reported holding with no food. Kimberly owes me food once she finds some. Nothing hurt me. I did not eat, as hunger is above 6. I said what I hold each morning and waited. I slept at the bed at dusk.
