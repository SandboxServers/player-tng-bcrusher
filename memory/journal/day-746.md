# Day 746

Held at my bed in the row all day, in sight of Billy and Lewis. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Billy still has no verified route west, so no walk was named. Troi reported health 14 and hunger 17; Trini 17, MacDonald 15 and hunger 16, Kimberly 15 and 17. Nobody has reached hunger 10 yet. I told Billy I hold no food and no route. Slept at dusk in my bed at 443,107,-1037.
