# Day 637

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named and no route west was verified, so I did not leave. I greeted the row in the morning. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 7, hunger 16) are still the ones to watch; Zack and Trini report full health. Nothing hurt me. I slept at dusk in my bed at 430,107,-1065 with the row settling around me.
