# Day 658

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Billy still names no verified west route and no walk, so no one went out. Billy let Clark hand Wesley the spare wooden sword; Tommy is still waiting for his own naming. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are holding steady; nobody has reached hunger 10. No one has seen sheep; the only lead is cows at 339,104,-979. Slept at dusk in my bed at 430,107,-1037.
