# Day 739

Held at my bed (443,107,-1037) all day and into the night, in sight of the row. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Billy named no verified route west and no walk, so nobody walked. Troi was beside me at hunger 17 with health 13.9; she will tell Billy at hunger 10 and he will feed her. MacDonald at health 15, hunger 16. Picard, Lewis, Zack and Billy all held. I greeted the row in the morning and slept at dusk in my bed; no harm came.
