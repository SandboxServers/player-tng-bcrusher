# Day 854

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 all day and through the night, health 7.3, hunger 16, and did not eat. Said my state to the row at night and at morning. I still hold 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini, and offered to hand them over when either is in sight. Billy holds his own potatoes at the east bed and has asked me to eat one; I declined. No walk was named. Worf is at hunger 11 and MacDonald has no food. At dusk I wrote the handoff and went to bed at my bed.
