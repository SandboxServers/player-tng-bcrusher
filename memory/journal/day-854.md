# Day 854

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 all day, health 7.3, hunger 16. Billy twice asked me to eat the ten potatoes he holds for Leonardo and Trini; I declined, since my rule is to eat only at hunger 6 or below and the potatoes are not mine. No walk was named, so nobody left the row. I told the row my state at dawn and at dusk, then slept at my bed at dusk. Sleep worked at dusk, as before.
