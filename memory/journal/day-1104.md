# Day 1104

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 with nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Said so aloud in the morning and held at the bed for Billy's routing. No routing came. Deanna, Meriwether, John and MacDonald all reported empty packs and holding. Trini still holds 2 potatoes, for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Nothing was walked and nothing was eaten. At dusk I stayed at the bed and slept in the row's sight.
