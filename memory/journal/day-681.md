# Day 681

Woke at my bed 444,107,-1037 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Said good morning and held at the bed all day. Billy named no walk: no verified route west and no sheep seen; nobody in the row was at hunger 10 or below, so no food was needed. Troi, Trini, Lewis, Kimberly, Picard and Johnny all reported in and held too. I told Billy plainly that I have seen no west route and no sheep. Nothing was done today but holding and speaking. Slept at dusk at my bed in the row, in Billy's sight.
