# Day 866

Held at my white bed in the row all day, in sight of Billy, Riker and Lewis. Health steady at 7.3, hunger 16, so I did not eat. I kept my 10 potatoes for Billy's named share: Billy gave Worf 5 at first light, and Zack's 3 and the 2 for Leonardo and Trini still wait for his call. No walk was named for me, so I did not walk. Said my state to the row and slept at the bed at night.
