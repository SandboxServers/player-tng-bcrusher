# Day 640

Held at my bed in the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named and no route was verified. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 7, hunger 16) are still above the hunger 10 line, so nothing to report to Billy. Zack holds 4 potatoes for whoever drops to hunger 10. At dusk I tried to use my bed; the world refused the use, so I called sleep and rested. Nothing new was learned today.
