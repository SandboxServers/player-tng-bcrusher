# Day 750

Held at my bed in the row (443,107,-1037) all day. Health 20, hunger 20, no food held. No verified west route and no sheep reported; Billy named no walk. Heard Billy, Trini, Troi, Kimberly, MacDonald, Picard say the same: hold until Billy names a walk. At dusk I said goodnight, sat near Kimberly, Picard, Trini and Lewis, and slept. Sleep worked 9 of 10 needed. Nothing was hurt, nothing was lost.
