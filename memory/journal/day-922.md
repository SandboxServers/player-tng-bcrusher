# Day 922

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 at night, health 19.3, hunger 17, holding nothing. The row held at the beds all day with no food and no walk named by Billy. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf. Signs at the beds read that Kimberly and MacDonald were shot on day 1061, so the danger is real. I said what I hold and asked Billy for routing; he has not answered. No walk taken. Slept at the bed at dusk.
