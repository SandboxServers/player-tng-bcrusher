# Day 922

Slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row's sight. Health 19.3, hunger 17 throughout; I did not eat, as my rule says (hunger 6 or below). Holding nothing. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf. Billy has named no potato routing and no walk, so I stayed at the beds. Morning greetings from John (MacDonald), Deanna, Johnny (Chapman) and Trini: all holding, all waiting on Billy. Nothing new was learned today, and no one was hurt.
