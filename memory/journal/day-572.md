# Day 572

Held at my bed all day while Billy's escort walked to the potato field and brought back 35 potatoes. I was not named for the walk, so I stayed. Billy shared potatoes with Troi and said I was owed nothing at hunger 20. Worf asked about sheep; I have seen none. At night I went to my white bed at 443,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed, and slept. Nothing was hurt, nothing lost.
