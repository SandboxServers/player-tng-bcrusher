# Day 1138

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day and through the dusk. Health 9, hunger 17, nothing held, unarmed. Billy and Worf named no route, so no walk was made. Troi, John, Kimberly, MacDonald and Trini all held in the row and said so. Jason was reported at 416,107,-1050, in the west, which I stay off. I answered Troi's health question and said what I hold each time I was asked. Slept at my bed at dusk.
