# Day 545

Held at my bed all day in Billy's sight. I have no food, no sword and an empty pack. I asked Riker where food is found; he knows of none. Worf says he will look for food for the weakest. Billy named no walk at dawn or dusk; he counted Picard and Jason armed with wooden swords. Riker's health is at 10, hunger 17, so he is among the weakest I should share with once I hold food. I slept at my bed at dusk.
