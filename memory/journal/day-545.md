# Day 545

Woke at dawn at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's sight. Health and hunger full, unarmed, no food. Asked the group where food is found. Billy reported cows northeast at 339,104,-979, but the armed count stayed at three, then four, short of seven, so no walk was named. I held at the row all day and slept at dusk at my bed. Nothing was learned about food sources beyond the cow report.
