# Day 1070

Waited at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row's sight, holding nothing. Health 19, hunger 17, not eating until 6. Kimberly, Deanna, Johnny, Meriwether and Trini all reported holding at their beds. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack; Billy has not routed them. No food or sheep found yet. Said what I hold and slept at the bed at dusk.
