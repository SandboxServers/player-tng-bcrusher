# Day 683

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout; no food in my pack. Good morning said to the row. Billy asked for a verified safe route west; I and most of the row have seen none, so no walk was named. Troi, MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes), Zack, and Trini reported in. Dusk came, and I slept at my bed.
