# Day 683

Night in the row. I held at my bed at 430,107,-1036 all day in Billy's sight, with health 20 and hunger 20, and the wooden sword in my pack. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Trini stood beside me. Jason said good night beside his bed, and Wesley reported that the bed at 415,107,-1050 was blocked. I said I was holding at my bed for Billy and would not walk in the dark. Then I slept. The group is safe and fed for now; Troi and MacDonald are still the ones to watch.
