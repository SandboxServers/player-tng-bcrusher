# Day 683

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk on day 682 or 683. The row talked each morning: MacDonald at health 11 and hunger 16 with three potatoes, Troi at health 14 and hunger 16, Trini at 17. Nobody needed me to hand out food, and I hold none. Cows were sighted near 339,104,-979, but no sheep yet. Slept at night in my bed at 430,107,-1036 with no errand running.
