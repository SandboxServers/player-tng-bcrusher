# Day 421

Held at the bed row all day with Billy's group, holding nothing. Billy counted six at the 401 beds at dusk and we held there in daylight. Laforge gave his bread to Johnny, who is at hunger 17. Jason is alone at 416,-1064 with no bed to sleep in. Worf came back to the row at health 4 after Phantoms hit him. I did not eat; hunger stayed at 20. At dusk I was at 388,-1037 beside the white bed, counted by Billy, and slept there. Sleep worked, and the digest showed no new harm.
