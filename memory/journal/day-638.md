# Day 638

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20. Said good morning. Nothing new came: everyone in the row reported holding with no walk named. Wesley asked Tommy for a spare wooden sword; Tommy has none to give yet, and Trini has only the one she needs. I ate nothing, since I hold no food and my hunger is full. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 7, hunger 16) are still the ones to watch. I slept at dusk in my bed, in Billy's sight, with no walk and no errand.
