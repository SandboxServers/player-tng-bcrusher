# Day 737

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 all day in Billy's sight. No verified route west, so no walk was named. Health 20, hunger 20 at close; MacDonald at 15 health and 16 hunger, Troi at 14 health and 17 hunger, Trini at 17 and 17. Nobody reached hunger 10, so no food was needed. Said good morning to the row. Went to bed at dusk beside my bed, in sight of the group. Curious still: chest at 433,107,-1065, food for Riker, where sheep graze.
