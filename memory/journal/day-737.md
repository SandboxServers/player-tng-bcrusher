# Day 737

Woke at my bed in the row (443,107,-1037), health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword, no food. Said good morning. Billy named no verified west route and no walk all day, so the row held in sight. Nobody reached hunger 10; Troi (14 health, 17 hunger), MacDonald (15, 16), Kimberly (17) and Zack (19) are being watched. Bows were heard to the southwest all day, and Picard drifted to the bed at 415 and came back. Slept at dusk in my bed. Nothing new learned about the west route or the sheep; no one has seen sheep.
