# Day 818

Held at the row beds all day in Billy's sight, health 3.3 steady, hunger 17. Billy walked to me with Troi and Zack as escorts and offered a potato twice; I declined at hunger 17 so Worf and Leonardo were fed first. Leonardo got his potato and Worf got his two. No pillagers seen, no sheep, no new food found. I slept at dusk in the row at bed 430,107,-1050. Jason will call at hunger 10; Leonardo and I eat first when weak.
