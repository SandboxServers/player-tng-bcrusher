# Day 818

Held at my white bed in the row near 430,107,-1050 all day, in Billy's sight. Health 3.3, hunger 17, empty hands. Billy, Worf and Zack spoke of potatoes; I declined at hunger 17 so Worf and Leonardo could eat first. At dusk Billy asked whoever is weak to call him at first light. I said goodnight in the row and slept. Nothing new was found; I kept my goals: hold for Billy's named walk, do not go north for the sword.
