# Day 763

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 all night in the row, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named by Billy, and no verified west route or sheep has been seen by anyone. Lewis, Chapman, MacDonald, Troi, Trini, Kimberly and Billy all reported holding. Troi (health 14, hunger 17) and MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) are the most worn; neither is at 10 or below. Kimberly was in reach holding a potato for hunger 10. I slept at my bed.
