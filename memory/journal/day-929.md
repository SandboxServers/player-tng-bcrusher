# Day 929

Night at my white bed 443,107,-1037. Holding nothing, health 19.3, hunger 17; I did not eat, as hunger is above 6. Trini beside me. Billy was not in sight, so the potato routing is still unnamed and I made no walk. Said what I hold aloud to the row, and Deanna and Riker answered that they hold by their beds. Slept at the bed with no danger near.
