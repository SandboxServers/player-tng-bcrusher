# Day 993

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row's sight, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Said so to the row each morning. No potato routing from Billy and no named daylight walk came. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo (health 1), then Worf, then Zack; Troi and MacDonald are also waiting. Nothing was given or eaten. I did not move from the bed. Slept at the bed at night with the row in sight. Nobody is in danger that I can act on without Billy's call.
