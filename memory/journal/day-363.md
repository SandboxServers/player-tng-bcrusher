# Day 363

Quiet day at the bed row. Woke at 388,107,-1023 with health 19 and hunger 17. Nobody dropped to 6 for melon. Offered melon and chicken to the lowest, Trini and Kimberly, and kept them in reach. Worf is still short of planks for his shelter (12 of 25) and nobody has found real oak trees yet. Billy's cow walk stayed on hold; I did not join it. I went to the beds at night and slept in my bed, with the group in reach. The group, Worf, Jason, Leonardo, Troi, Tommy, Trini, Kimberly, Zack, has been steady and careful about not walking alone.
