# Day 568

Held at my bed at 443,107,-1065 all day in the row's sight. Billy named Jason and Picard for a log search north of the row; it was blocked, and no logs were found. No walk was named for me, so I stayed put. Health held at 20 and hunger at 20. My pack is empty and I am unarmed with no food. At night I sent a short word that I was holding for first light and went to sleep at my bed. Wesley holds 5 potatoes, and Riker is still hungry. Nothing new was learned about gives or the chest at 447,107,-1065.
