# Day 1093

Quiet day at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, and I did not eat (not yet 6). No walk was named and no routing came from Billy. Trini still holds 2 potatoes at bed 430 for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. Kimberly and Johnny are waiting for Billy's call, and Lewis, Troi and MacDonald report no food. I said what I hold in the morning. At dusk I slept at my bed in the row; the sleep was partial (6 of 10 asleep) when I stopped.
