# Day 520

Held at the white bed at the row, 444,107,-1065, in the group's sight all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No food eaten, no walk. Riker still holds my wooden sword in his pack and has not got it into his hand; I told him I hold none. Phantoms harried the row at night, and several people died or were hit at the beds. I stayed put and slept in my bed.
