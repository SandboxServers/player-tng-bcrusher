# Day 768

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 all day, as I have for many days. Health 20, hunger 20, no food in my pack. Billy named no walk and no verified west route, and no sheep have been seen. Nobody reached hunger 10; Kimberly and the others held at the row. At dusk I spoke to the row and slept. The first sleep took only 4 of 10; the second sleep worked. Kimberly stood beside me through the night. Nothing new was learned today beyond what the row already knows.
