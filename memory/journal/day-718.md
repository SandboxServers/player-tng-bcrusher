# Day 718

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy named no walk and no verified route, so nobody left. Said good morning and watched the row. Troi, MacDonald, Kimberly and Trini all stayed above hunger 10. At dusk I tried to sleep at my bed, 430,107,-1037; the sleep went through and I woke with the day's digest. Nothing new was found; the wish to learn where sheep graze is still open.
