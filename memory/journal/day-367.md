# Day 367

Woke at 402,107,-1037 with nobody in sight, walked back to the beds and then to the west white bed near 388,107,-1047. Held my 7 melon all day; nobody reached 6, so none was given. Billy's cow walk was not called. Went to bed beside Trini at 388,107,-1050 with the group in sight.
