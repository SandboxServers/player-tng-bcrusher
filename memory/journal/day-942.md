# Day 942

Woke at my white bed in the row, holding nothing, health 19.3, hunger 17 (not eating until 6 or below). Said aloud what I hold and asked Billy for potato routing twice; he has not answered. Nobody in the row has named a daylight walk or food run. Deanna, John, Lewis, Trini (health 11) and I all held in place. Trini's 2 potatoes stay for Leonardo first. I slept at dusk in the row's sight and woke to a new day. Still no food or sheep known.
