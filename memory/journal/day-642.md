# Day 642

Held at my bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Lewis spoke from his bed at dusk: he too is sleeping for the night, no walk. I said goodnight to the row and slept at my bed; the sleep worked. Tommy and MacDonald were not in my sight, so nothing to report on their hunger. Still curious about the chest at 433,107,-1065, food for Riker, and oak logs and sheep. Nothing new learned today beyond the hold.
