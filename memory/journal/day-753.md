# Day 753

Woke at my bed at 443,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20. Said good morning to the row. Billy named no walk and no verified route west, so I held at the bed in his sight all day. Chapman, Picard, Lewis and Laforge held too, each with no food to share yet. Billy asked for anyone at hunger 10 or below to call him; nobody did. Lewis's hunger eased to 19. At night I said goodnight beside my bed, then slept. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's steady holding.
