Day 319. Woke at the beds, 402,107,-1079, health 20, empty pack. Said my position and offered to join anyone going for animals. Chapman found a cow at 370,93,-1124 past a skeleton to the north-west. I have no sword, so I agreed to go only in a pair with Macdonald, who got Chapman's wooden sword through Worf. Data was boxed in by oak doors and had no food; Troi and Clark offered melon at first light. Night came; I slept beside the beds in view of the group.
