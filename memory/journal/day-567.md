# Day 567

Held at my bed at 443,107,-1065 the whole day, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed, no food. Billy named no walk for me, so I did not walk. I said plainly that my pack is empty. Wesley is the hungriest we know of (hunger 18, holding 7 potatoes and staying put for Billy's call). Deanna holds 13 potatoes. Tommy and Kimberly hold too. Nothing was found to eat and no sword is in reach. I slept at night in the group's sight.
