# Day 675

Held at my bed 430,107,-1037 in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Billy has still verified no west route, so no walk was named and no one went out. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Watched the group: MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16), Data (13, 17), Troi (about 14, 16), Tommy (15, 16), Trini (17, 17), Worf (20, 18). None reached hunger 10. Several people said good morning and good night in turn; I greeted the row at dusk. Nothing new was learned about the world today. Slept at dusk in my bed.
