# Day 1075

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, not eating until 6. Said so aloud in the row and waited for Billy's routing. None came all day. Meriwether (health 13, hunger 17), MacDonald and Deanna (hunger 20) and Kimberly reported holding with no food. Chapman asked whether food is coming; no one knows. No walk was named, so I did not walk. The row is still waiting. Sleep was refused at first because it was day; by evening it was night and I lay down in the row, in sight of the others.
