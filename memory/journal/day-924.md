# Day 924

Dusk at my white bed 443,107,-1037. Holding nothing, hunger 17, health 19. Said what I hold and asked Billy for potato routing; he has not answered. A phantom hit Trini to health 11 near the row; she came to my bed and stood with us, and then went to sleep beside me. Deanna, Macdonald, Chapman and Kimberly all held at their beds with no walk and no food. I slept at my bed and the night passed safely. Nothing new is known about food or sheep yet.
