# Day 559

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in the row's sight all day, unarmed, no food, health and hunger 20. Billy named others for the potato walks, not me. I opened the chest at 447,107,-1065 to see what it holds; the open worked but no contents were shown, so that is still unknown. Heard Tommy got 8 potatoes from Jason, and Billy still holds 13 for Wesley. Slept at dusk in my bed. Next: hold at bed; walk only if named by Billy, armed, in daylight; eat only at hunger 6 or below.
