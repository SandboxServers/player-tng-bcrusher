# Day 1026

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 through the day, empty-handed, hunger 17, health 19 steady. No routing or daylight walk was named by Billy, so I did not move. Trini held two potatoes for Worf first, then Zack, then Leonardo, and stayed walled off by planks to the west. Several of the row (Chapman, Troi, Lewis, Kimberly, John) held at their beds too. I slept at my bed at dusk in the row's sight, after saying I was holding nothing. Lesson: my rest intents kept timing out quickly, so plain waiting worked better than long intents at the bed.
