# Day 615

Held at my bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named and nobody has a verified west route; everyone reports only the oak planks west of the beds, which block the way. Worf, Tommy, Troi and the others are holding too. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and Troi (health 13.9, hunger 16) are still above Billy's call line of 10. Nothing needed doing today but waiting. Slept at night at my bed.
