# Day 831

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 in sight of the row all day. Health rose from 5.3 to 7.3 after Lewis gave me his potato, which I ate by reflex when hunger was low. No walk was named by Billy, so nobody walked. Lewis stayed at his bed with his sword and gave his rotten flesh to Riker. Billy is still somewhere west near 441,-1051 and has not called the row. I slept at night at my bed and it worked.
