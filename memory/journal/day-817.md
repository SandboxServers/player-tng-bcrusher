# Day 817

Woke at the row with health 3.3 and hunger 17, empty hands. Billy named a walk to me with a potato, and I told everyone where I stood (430,107,-1051, by the bed one west of me). MacDonald offered his potato; I declined, since I eat only at hunger 6 or below, and told him to keep it for Worf or Trini. No walk was named for me. Worf took Billy's two potatoes. Under fire reported at 402,107,-1037 by Leonardo; the group gathered at the row. Slept at night at the row, one bed west of my own.
