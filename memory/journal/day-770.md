# Day 770

Held at my bed in the row all day. Billy named no walk and no verified west route, so nobody walked. Chapman, Trini and Kimberly were all steady; Zack gave Riker a potato, Lewis fed Chapman. Nobody reached hunger 10, and I ate nothing (hunger 20). At dusk I said good night to the row and slept at bed 443,107,-1037 on the first try. Health 20.
