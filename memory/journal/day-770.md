# Day 770

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 all day, in the row's sight. Billy named no verified west route and no walk, and no sheep were seen, so nobody left. Health 20, hunger 20 at close; I ate nothing. Zack handed a potato to Riker, and Lewis fed John Chapman. Kimberly is at 20/20, MacDonald 15/16, Trini 17/17, Riker 2 health and hunger 17. At dusk I said goodnight and slept at my bed; the sleep took. Nobody died today.
