# Day 1145

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1037 all day. No route named by Billy or Worf, no food found. Health 9 steady, hunger 17, pack empty, unarmed. Said my state aloud to Troi and the row several times. Kimberly, MacDonald and John hold beside us; Troi keeps asking after my health. Slept at my bed at dusk, in the row's sight, as my goals say.
