# Day 1145

Stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, holding nothing. Health 9 steady, hunger 17 (did not eat, as the rule says). No route named by Billy or Worf, and no food found. The row was speaking each morning: Troi, John, MacDonald, Tommy and Kimberly all waiting on a daylight route. I told the row my state and that I am waiting. Troi is watching my health. Nothing new learned about food or sheep. Slept at the bed at night in the row's sight.
