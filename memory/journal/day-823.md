# Day 823

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 all day, health 5.3, hunger 17, empty hands. Billy named no walk, so nobody went north for the sword. I greeted the row at morning and dusk and declined the offered potatoes from Worf and MacDonald, since I eat only at hunger 6 or below and they are kept for the weakest. Many in the row hold at their beds unarmed, hungry at 17. Slept at my bed at night; the sleep worked.
