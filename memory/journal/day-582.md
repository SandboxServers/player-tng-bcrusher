# Day 582

Held at my bed all day, unarmed, in the row's sight. Billy named no walk. Tommy found solid oak planks blocking the path west from his bed. Tommy has two spare wooden swords and offered one once I stand beside him in the row, in Billy's sight. I asked for it after Worf is served, and I will wait for Billy's word before I leave my bed. Slept at dusk at my white bed 430,107,-1065.
