# Day 803

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, no food in my pack. Billy named no verified west route and no walk, so no one walked. Trini is at health 10, hunger 17, first in line when food turns up. Data at 13/17, Worf 13 hunger, Jason 17, MacDonald 15/16. Nobody holds food. An enderman stood near Data, so Data stayed put. At dusk Billy and the others went to bed in sight of the row. I slept at my bed 415,107,-1037.
