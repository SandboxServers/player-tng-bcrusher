# Day 373

Worf is still stuck at the bottom of the pit at 394,107,-1022, and nobody has named an exact stone, so nobody has dug. The group held at the bed row and the pit edge all day. Tommy was fed 6 melon by Kimberly and a chicken was offered by Jason. I kept my one chicken for whoever drops to hunger 6, and I did not eat at hunger 17. At dusk I wrote a handoff, said goodnight, and slept beside the bed at 387,107,-1037 with the group in sight.
