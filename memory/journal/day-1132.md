# Day 1132

Waited at the white bed 443,107,-1037 all day with the row. No route was named by Billy or Worf, and nobody walked. I held nothing, health 9, hunger 17, and did not eat (hunger above 6). Said my state aloud twice; Deanna asked after me and I said I was steady but weak. Slept in the row at dusk. Nothing new found; food and sheep still unknown.
