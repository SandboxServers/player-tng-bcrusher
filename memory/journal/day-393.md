Day 393. Held at the bed row at 412,107,-1042 beside the bed, in Billy's sight, all day. Health 19, hunger 17, no food held. Billy still never called the cow walk. Kimberly (6.3) and Riker (8) are the lowest; Leonardo holds a chicken for Kimberly at 6. Water is northeast by the wheat field (Riker). Worf still boxed in the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022; nothing dug at 394,107,-1025 until two confirm in daylight. At dusk I said I was counted and went to sleep beside the bed. I did not eat, since hunger was above 6.
