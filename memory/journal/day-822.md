# Day 822

Held at my bed 430,107,-1051 all day, as Billy asked. Health stayed steady at 5.3, hunger 17, empty hands; I declined food (Zack's potato, earlier Troi's) since hunger was above 6. Billy's escorted walk to the NE potato patch went out with Jason and Worf at dusk; I did not go. No sheep seen by Troi or Worf; only cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. The row is steady, with MacDonald, Lewis, Kimberly and Laforge holding at their beds. I slept at my bed at night.
