# Day 762

Woke at the row beside my bed, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword only, no food. Said good morning to Kimberly and Trini, who were in reach. Billy asked again for a verified route west past x=370 and for sheep; nobody had either, so the row held with no walk. Kimberly (health 15, hunger 17), Trini (17) and MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) are all still above the call line of 10. Chapman is trying to get his sword into hand on open ground. At dusk I walked to my bed at 443,107,-1037, said goodnight, and slept. Nothing was hurt or lost today.
