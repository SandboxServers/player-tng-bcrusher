# Day 419

Woke at the row at 430,-1037 with nothing, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody was in sight there, so I walked back to 417,-1037 and held with Billy, Leonardo, Trini, Picard, Kimberly, Tommy in reach. Billy never called the cow walk. Riker reported health 10 and I had no food to send him. Worf got out of the pit at dusk and walked to the row. At night I stood beside the bed at the row and slept; the sleep ran and I woke on day 420 with nothing new. Nothing was lost today.
