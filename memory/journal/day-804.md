# Day 804

Held at my bed 415,107,-1037 all day, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy named me for a NE walk with Jason; I said I would go armed, but I did not move, and Billy walked it with Zack's sword, returning with 14 potatoes. The potatoes went first to Trini (hunger 17, health 10), then Troi, MacDonald and Zack. I stayed put and did not eat. Slept at dusk in Billy's sight.
