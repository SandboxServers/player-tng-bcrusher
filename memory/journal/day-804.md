# Day 804

Woke at bed 415,107,-1037 with health 20, hunger 20, holding only a wooden sword. No food, bow or route to 463,107,-1073 was found by anyone in the row, so Billy named no walk and I held at the bed all day, in sight of the row. I said good morning and reported my state. Trini (health 10, hunger 17) is first in line for food when any shows. Riker was at health 6; Lewis holds the rotten flesh for him, within reach. Worf (hunger 13) and MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) are being watched. Night came; I slept at the bed beside Billy.
