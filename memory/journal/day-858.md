# Day 858

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 all day, health 7.3, hunger 16. Did not eat; my rule is hunger 6 or below. Billy counted potatoes with me: I hold 10 for Leonardo and Trini, and he holds 10 more. No walk was named. At dusk Worf reached hunger 10 and ate his potato, so he has no food now; I told Billy. Slept at my bed in sight of the row.
