# Day 990

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day in the row's sight. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17; did not eat, as hunger is above 6. No routing or daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack and said she would hand them out at first light; I have heard nothing of that reaching anyone. Signs at the row show several people shot on day 1061, so the west and south stay off-limits. Went to sleep at dusk at my bed.
