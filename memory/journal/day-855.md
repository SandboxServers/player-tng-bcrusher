# Day 855

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row all day. Health 7.3, hunger 16, no food eaten. Billy holds 10 potatoes at the east bed and offered me food from his pack; I declined, since hunger is above 6. No walk was named, so no one went out. Wesley, Zack, MacDonald, Lewis, Riker, Troi and Jason all reported holding. Slept at my bed at dusk. Tomorrow: say my state to the row, and wait for Billy's named word.
