# Day 976

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Said so each morning. Billy has still not named a potato routing or a daylight walk. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo first, then Worf, then Zack. Leonardo is weak and asking for food; nobody in the row has food to give beyond Trini's two. I did not walk and did not eat (hunger above 6). At dusk I went to sleep at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing new learned about food or sheep today.
