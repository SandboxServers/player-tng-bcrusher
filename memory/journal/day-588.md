# Day 588

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1065 all day in Billy's sight. Pack empty, unarmed, no food; health and hunger stayed at 20. Billy named no walk, and no one needed food from me. West route to Worf's bed is still blocked by oak planks. Troi (health 13.9, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are still hurt and hungry; I have nothing to give. I said plainly what I hold in the morning, as my goal asks. Slept at dusk in my bed.
