# Day 798

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword only. Billy still names no verified west route, no food and no sheep, so no walk. Trini at 430 is at health 9 and hunger 17; Billy has the row stay at the beds because bows are drawn to the south. Riker has no food yet; Troi offered her potato, and Lewis gave his rotten flesh. Nobody has reached hunger 10 except none so far. Slept at dusk at bed 415,107,-1037.
