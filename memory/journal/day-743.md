# Day 743

Woke at my bed in the row (443,107,-1037), health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword, no food. Nobody was near at first. Said good morning and held. Billy asked the row for a verified route west; nobody had one, so no walk was named. Everyone in the row held at their beds: Billy and Picard at 20 and 20, Lewis at 20 and 19, Trini at 17 and 17, Troi at 13.9 and 17, MacDonald at 15 and 16 near 421,107,-1040. Troi said she would call Billy at hunger 10. I told Billy plainly what I hold: no route, no food, and I am holding in sight. No one reached hunger 10. At night I slept in my bed in the row, and I did no walks today.
