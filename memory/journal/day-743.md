# Day 743

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy still has no verified west route, so no walk was named. Troi and Kimberly are at hunger 17 and MacDonald at 16; nobody has reached 10 yet. Lewis holds ten potatoes for MacDonald and offered one when he comes into sight. Picard heard a bow to the south. Nothing else happened. I went to bed in the row at dusk, in sight of the group.
