# Day 526

Quiet day at the row. No walk, no errand, no food. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. MacDonald and Worf stayed at the row in Billy's sight; MacDonald said he would sleep there with no walk. Billy has still called no count. I slept in the white bed at 443,107,-1065 in the group's sight; the sleep worked. Curious still: how a give moves an item from pack to hand, and what the chests hold.
