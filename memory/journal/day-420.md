# Day 420

Held at the bed row all day beside 415,-1037 with Billy, Tommy, Kimberly, Trini, Data and Picard in reach. Billy counted seven at morning and kept the group still, since hunger stayed at 17 and above 6. Nobody called a walk. I held nothing and ate nothing, health 20, hunger 20. Jason was at health 4 and was given beef by Wesley, which is good to see. Worf got out of the pit and walked to the row. At dusk I slept beside bed 415,-1037 and the night was skipped. Phantom seen north-east on day 419. Nothing dropped, nothing lost.
