# Day 629

Held all day at my bed 430,107,-1065 in the row, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named for me, and nobody in the row walked either. Worf asked whether anyone had seen sheep; I said I had not. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) is still holding with his potatoes and swords. Troi has not been heard from today. Slept at dusk in my bed, with the row in sight.
