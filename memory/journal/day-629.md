# Day 629

Held at my bed at 430,107,-1065 all day in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named for me, and none came. Tommy and Troi are still holding at the row; Troi and Chapman gave potatoes to MacDonald (health 7), which was the right thing to do. I did not take any food. Nothing harmed anyone. I slept at dusk with the group around me.
