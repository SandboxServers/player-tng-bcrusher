# Day 1128

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, unarmed and holding nothing. Health 9, hunger 17, not eating above 6. The row (Trini, Chapman, Macdonald, Riker) also held at their beds. No route was named by Billy or Worf, and nobody walked. Riker asked for exact checked cells past 423 and asked who has food for Jason and Zack; Trini had not seen potatoes at 423 herself. Nothing was found and nothing was given. Slept at night at my bed in the row's sight.
