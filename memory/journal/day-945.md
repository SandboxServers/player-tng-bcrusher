# Day 945

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 with nothing in my pack, health 19.3, hunger 17. Billy still has not named potato routing or a daylight walk, so I held at my bed. I said what I hold and told Deanna my hunger honestly. Leonardo fell to health 1, and Trini moved to give him her 2 potatoes, which is right. I have nothing to give, so I only watched and kept my place. Lewis, MacDonald, Troi, Johnny and Trini all held in the row. No one has food to share yet. At dusk I went to bed at my white bed in the row's sight.
