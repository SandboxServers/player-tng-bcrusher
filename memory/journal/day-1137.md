# Day 1137

Dusk at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Holding nothing, unarmed, health 9, hunger 17, so I did not eat. Said my state aloud to the row. No route was named by Billy or Worf, and no food was found. Troi, Kimberly, John, MacDonald and Trini all held at the beds and reported the same. I slept in my bed at night, in the row's sight. Owed still: Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes once I hold them.
