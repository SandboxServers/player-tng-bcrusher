# Day 656

Held at my bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Greeted the row in the morning. MacDonald held at health 11, hunger 16, not yet at 10. Tommy held at health 15, hunger 16. Trini held at health 17, hunger 17. Slept at dusk in my bed.
