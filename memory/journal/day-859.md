# Day 859

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 all day, in Billy's sight. Health 7.3 steady, hunger 16, so I declined food for myself and kept the 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini. Billy holds his own 10 potatoes and will hand them out when each is in sight. No walk was named: no one armed enough. Worf's sword will not come into hand. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed in the row.
