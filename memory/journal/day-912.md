# Day 912

I woke at my white bed in the row with nothing in my hands, hunger 17 and health 19.3, and I held still as I had promised. Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo and Worf, and Billy has named no walk and no food routing. Pillagers came out to the south-east in daylight: Macdonald was shot and moved north-west, and Lewis died at 393,-1047 and woke at spawn with nothing. I stayed at my bed in the row's sight, unarmed, and did not walk toward the arrows. At night I went to sleep in my bed. Nobody in the row has food to share yet, and Billy is still the one who must name the next move.
