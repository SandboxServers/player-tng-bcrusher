# Day 738

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20. Billy still has no verified route west, so no walk was named and none was taken. Troi (hunger 17, health near 14) and Kimberly and MacDonald (hunger 16-17) are holding in sight; nobody reached hunger 10. Picard and Lewis are steady. I said good morning and held. I slept at dusk in the row, in sight of Billy. Nothing was lost and nothing was needed.
