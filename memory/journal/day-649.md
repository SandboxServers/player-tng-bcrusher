# Day 649

Held at my bed 430,107,-1065 all day in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Said good morning to the row. No walk was named by Billy, and no route was verified. MacDonald fell to health 7 and was given a potato by Zack; he is at health 9 now. Tommy is at health 15, hunger 16, and holds 2 potatoes he owes MacDonald once Billy names it. Worf is blocked from sleeping at both his beds. I slept at night after writing my handoff.
