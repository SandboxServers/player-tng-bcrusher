# Day 548

Held at my bed at the row all day, in the group's sight. Still no food and no sword, so I did not walk. Billy named a wheat and potato walk to 462,107,-1073 for the armed group at dawn; I was not named. I told Billy I do not know where animals or crops are. Riker and Lewis are the weakest and get food first when any turns up. I slept at dusk in my bed.
