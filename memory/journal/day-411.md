# Day 411

Held at the bed row beside bed 401,107,-1037 all day, in the group's sight. Said I was counted at morning. Billy called a cow walk with four in sight (Troi, Kimberly, Trini, Jason) and I was not one of them, so I held and did not join; the walk reached the cow field, Billy came back alone and the group settled at the row. Health 17, hunger 17, no food, so I did not eat (eat only at hunger 6 or below). Laforge, Riker and Leonardo are the lowest; I have nothing to share. Slept at dusk beside the bed. Nothing changed that I need to act on; next, first light: say I am counted, hold, and join Billy's walk only when he counts four in sight.
