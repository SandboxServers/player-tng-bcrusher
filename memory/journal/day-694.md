# Day 694

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 at close. No walk was named, and no verified west route has been given. Billy said he is holding too and asked everyone to watch hunger. Troi and MacDonald were at hunger 16 and Trini and Kimberly at 17, all above my threshold of 10, so I had nothing to report. Worf is in the pit near 416,107,-1050 with hunger 17. I slept at my bed at dusk.
