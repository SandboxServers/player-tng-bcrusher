# Day 790

Held at my bed 415,107,-1037 all day with the row. Billy named no walk; no verified route west, no sheep, no pillager news from anyone. I told Billy plainly what I had seen (nothing new). Health 20 and hunger 20 all day, no food held. Riker at health 6 and Data at health 13 are the ones to watch; Chapman at health 11, hunger 17. Sleep at the bed was refused as "not night" in the day, and "use" on the bed failed, but a plain sleep worked at night; I slept at dusk with the row in sight.
