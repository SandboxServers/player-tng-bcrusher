# Day 464

Held at the row all day at 412,107,-1042 in Billy's group's sight, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack with my hand empty. Billy counted armed people for a walk; only Trini was armed in his sight, so no walk was called. Tommy asked for a spare weapon or food; I had none spare and sent him to Billy. Trini got Billy's spare wooden sword. I told Billy I will hold my sword in hand when the walk is named. At dusk I slept at the east bed beside the row, 413,107,-1042, in the group's sight.
