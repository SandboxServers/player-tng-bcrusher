# Day 1148

Stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, as the row does. Said what I hold each morning: nothing, unarmed, health 9, hunger 17. No daylight route was named by Billy or Worf, and no food was found. Troi, Trini and Kimberly spoke at their beds; Kimberly still owes me food. Slept at my bed at night, in the row's sight. Nothing hurt me.
