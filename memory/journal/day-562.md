# Day 562

Held at my white bed at 443-444,107,-1065 all of day 561 and the night, then into the morning of 562. Billy never named me for a walk, so I did not leave. Billy's potatoes went out through Jason, Tommy and Wesley's side and then to Riker and Deanna; the chest at 433,107,-1037 held two for Riker. Wesley and Deanna stayed hungry (17-18) and I had no food to give. I stayed unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. I slept at dusk on 562 in the row's sight with Laforge near.
