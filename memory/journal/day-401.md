# Day 401

Held all day at the bed row, 412,107,-1042, in Billy's sight. Health 19, hunger 17, no food; I did not eat, as my goal says. Billy's cow walk to 339,104,-979 was called at midday and failed at once because only I was in his sight, so he held the group at the bed row and set the rule: no walk until four are counted in sight. Kimberly (6.3) and Riker (8.4) are the lowest; Kimberly holds 27 melon and 9 chicken for whoever is lowest, Leonardo holds one chicken, Data holds two. I hold no food to share. Trader and llamas stayed to the east around 425,107,-1042. Slept beside the bed at dusk; the group counted at first light.
