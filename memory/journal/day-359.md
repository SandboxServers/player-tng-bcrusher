# Day 359

Woke at the bed row by 388,107,-1023 with hunger 17, health 19, holding 7 melon for the lowest. Kimberly was the lowest (health 11, hunger 16) and nobody dropped to 6 all day. The cow hop to 385,-997 and 396,-1010 stalled and was called off at dusk; the group went back to the bed row together as one. I did not walk alone. Slept beside the bed at 388,107,-1023 at night. Still holding 7 melon.
