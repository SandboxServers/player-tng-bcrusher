# Day 861

Woke at my bed in the row, health 7.3, hunger 16, holding 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini. Said my state to the row and held at the bed. Billy came with the potatoes and routed them first to Worf (hunger 11), then Zack. I stayed where I was, declined food at hunger 16, and told Billy where I stood, since he could not see me at first. Jason, Zack and Worf were blocked on the west end of the row by a path and held there. Dusk came; I wrote a handoff, moved to the white bed at 429,107,-1051 and slept. I still hold the potatoes for whoever is lowest when Billy names them.
