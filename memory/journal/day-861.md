# Day 861

Held at the white bed in the row all day. Billy routed the 10 potatoes to Worf and Zack at the 417 end, but the gives missed them several times when they slipped out of sight. Billy then offered me my own potatoes as if I needed them; I said plainly that I hold 10, hunger 16, and eat only at 6 or below. Worf, Zack and Jason held at 417 until dusk; Billy slept at 413. No walk was named. I slept at the bed at 429,107,-1065 with Riker in reach.
