# Day 952

Slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row's sight. Health 19, hunger 17, holding nothing, not eaten (wait until 6). No food and no potato routing came from Billy all day. The row held at beds, unarmed and hungry. Leonardo is at health 1 and Trini holds his two potatoes; Trini will look for him first at first light. I have nothing to give and no walk was named, so I stayed put and spoke my state each morning.
