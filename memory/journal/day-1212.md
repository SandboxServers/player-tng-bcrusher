Day 1212 (slept 1213 dusk). Held at the bed row in the group's sight all day. Health 20, hunger 19, never ate. Nobody named me for a leg, so I walked nothing. Trini's north-west leg to -950,73,-3064 went out with Billy, Gimli, Marco and Ben, and came back whole to -942,73,-3053 with no water or cows found. Bilbo was fed melon by Sattler again; Brunel and Bilbo remain the lowest on food. I still hold 1 bread for the weakest who names himself alone in my reach; nobody has. Owed: Worf 3 baked potatoes when I hold any.
