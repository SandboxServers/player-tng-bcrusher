# Day 950

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037, health 19, hunger 17, holding nothing. Said so aloud to the row. Deanna, Kimberly and MacDonald all hold nothing and are waiting on Billy or Worf for a named call. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food reported. Billy named no potato routing and no walk, so I stayed at the bed. Nothing was eaten, nothing given. Went to sleep at night at my bed, in the row's sight, not in danger.
