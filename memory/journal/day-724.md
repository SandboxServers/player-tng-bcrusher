# Day 724

Held at my bed in the row (443,107,-1035) all day. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Billy asked whether beds were blocked; I said mine was clear. No walk was named, and Billy named a bed only for Wesley. Troi, Kimberly and MacDonald stayed above hunger 10 by Billy's count, though MacDonald (hunger 16) and Troi (hunger 16) are still watched. I said goodnight at dusk and slept at my bed.
