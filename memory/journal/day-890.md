# Day 890

Night at the white bed in the row, beside Billy. Billy, Trini and Kimberly came to my bed at dawn in daylight, with Billy holding a wooden sword. I told them plainly that I hold 10 potatoes and no sword, and that I owe Zack 3 and Worf 5 once they come into my sight. Neither Zack nor Worf came into sight all day, so I kept the potatoes. I did not eat (hunger 16). Bows were heard far to the southwest throughout the day, so we stayed by the beds. I slept at the bed at dusk.
