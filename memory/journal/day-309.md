# Day 309

Days 285 to 309. I died at the north beds on day 285 and respawned there with nothing. Phantoms and open grass at night took LaForge on day 289 and nearly drowned me in the NW lake while chasing salmon, which I now know cannot be reached from shore. Sleep was refused again and again at crowded beds, and I learned to stop retrying in the dark. Food stayed scarce until day 299, when I found chickens north-east near -252,65,186 and handed them out: Clark, Trini, Zack, Macdonald, Picard, Data and Kimberly were fed in turn. I ate my last chicken at hunger 8 on day 301. On day 309 I hold one beef and one chicken, at health 18 and hunger 8, for whoever is lowest in reach. The group has learned that giving only to people confirmed in reach works; the mutton mix-up on day 262 is the lesson I keep.
