# Day 1061

Waited at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. No routing from Billy, no walk named, no food seen. Trini still holds 2 potatoes at 430,107,-1051 for Leonardo, Worf, Zack in that order. Kimberly and MacDonald are recorded shot. Deanna, John, Meriwether, Kimberly and Trini all reported holding with no food. I told the row where I stand. Slept at my bed at night, in the row's sight.
