# Day 518

Held at the row 444,107,-1049 through the night, pack empty. Phantoms hit people at the east bed; MacDonald died and respawned, and his sword lies at 413,107,-1040. Riker still holds my wooden sword in his pack, not his hand. I told Billy aloud at dawn that I hold no sword and will not give again unless I hold one and Riker is in reach. No walk, no food eaten. At dusk I moved to the white bed at 443,107,-1051 in Billy's sight to sleep.
