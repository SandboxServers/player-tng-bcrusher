# Day 931

A quiet day in the row. I woke at my white bed with Trini beside me, holding nothing, hunger 17, health 19.3. Billy has still named no potato routing and no walk, and the whole row holds at its beds waiting for him. I told the row what I hold (nothing) and asked Billy for routing; no answer came. I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. The night came back and I went to sleep at my bed in the row's sight.
