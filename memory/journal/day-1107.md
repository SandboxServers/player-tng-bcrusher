Day 1107. Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, as I have for days. Said what I hold (nothing) in the morning; no routing came from Billy. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Worf and Zack and is waiting for light to go to 423, but no cell was named. Leonardo and Meriwether are weak at health 13 and 1. Health 19, hunger 17, no food, so I did not eat. Slept at the bed in the row's sight.
