# Day 687

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20. The row held all day in Billy's sight. Billy named no route and no walk, and said bows were drawn to the southwest, so we stayed put. I answered Troi's morning question and said I was well. Trini (health 17, hunger 17) and Kimberly (hunger 17) are the ones to watch, and Zack offered potatoes to anyone who stands in his sight and asks. Nothing hurt me. I slept at dusk at my bed.
