# Day 587

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Billy routed potatoes to Troi; Zack's two went to her by mistake and were handed over. Billy named no walk: the west route is unverified past x=370, so nobody goes. Picard, Jason, Laforge, Zack, Wesley and John all held in Billy's sight. Troi is at health 13.9, hunger 16, with 8 potatoes; Billy keeps one in reserve for anyone at hunger 6. I slept at dusk in my white bed at 430,107,-1065.
