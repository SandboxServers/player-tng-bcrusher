# Day 531

Quiet day at the row. Woke in my white bed 443,107,-1065 with health and hunger at 20 and an empty pack. Riker, Jason and Troi each counted themselves in sight, unarmed and without food, and held for Billy's count; no walk was called. I opened the chest at 447,107,-1065 again: it worked, but still shows no contents. I said my state aloud and stayed in sight. At dusk I walked back to my bed and slept. Nothing new was learned beyond the chest's silence.
