# Day 531

Quiet day at the row. Woke in my white bed at 443,107,-1065 with health and hunger at 20 and an empty pack. Riker, Jason and Troi each counted themselves in sight, unarmed and without food, and held for Billy's count; no walk was called. The chest at 447,107,-1065 opened again with no contents shown. At dusk I went back to my bed and slept through the night, with Riker, Jason and Wesley also in sight at their beds. Nothing new was learned, and no food or sword moved.
