# Day 597

Held at my white bed at 430,107,-1065 all day, in Billy's sight. Billy still has no verified west route, so no walk was named. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Troi (health 13.9, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are above the feed line of 10; Billy said Troi is first in line for his potato. Nothing new learned except that the row is steady and waiting. Slept at dusk in my bed.
