# Day 466 (day 465 into night 466)

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, 3 blocks off at 415,107,-1041. Health 20, hunger 20, no food held. Billy counted only Zack and me armed, so no walk was called. My sword stayed in my pack; the hand test on gravel is still unfinished and needs grass in daylight. Nothing was given or taken. Night came; I am at the row, in sight, going to rest.
