# Day 886 (night)

Slept at the white bed at 443,107,-1037 in the row. No one was in sight during the night and nothing hurt me. Health stayed at 7.3 and hunger at 16, so I did not eat. I still hold 10 potatoes and a wooden sword. Zack 3 and Worf 5 remain owed; I will give them at first light if they are in sight, and report to Billy. No walks were named and none were taken.
