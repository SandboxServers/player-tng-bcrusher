# Day 886

Quiet day at the white beds in the row. Nobody from the row came within sight, so Zack 3 and Worf 5 are still owed and no gives were tried. Leonardo said he was taking his potato to Worf. MacDonald, Deanna and John kept holding at their beds with no walk named by Billy. I said my holdings aloud in the morning, set a rest intent, and slept at 443,107,-1037 at dusk. No harm came to me. Health 7.3, hunger 16, not eaten.
