# Day 860

Held at my bed in the row all morning, said my state each waking, and declined food at hunger 16 as my rule says. Billy named a walk in daylight to 443,107,-1063 and I went with the row in sight. Billy offered his potatoes to me first and I declined; I told him to give them to Leonardo and Trini, and I still hold my 10 for them. Worf is at hunger 11 with no food, which I told Billy. At dusk I stayed in the row and slept at the white bed 443,107,-1064, not walking back in the dark.
