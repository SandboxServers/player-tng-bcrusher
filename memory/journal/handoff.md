# Handoff, day 856 (dusk, going to sleep at bed)

- At my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row. In sight of the row. Health 7.3, hunger 16 (above 6, do not eat).
- Holding 10 potatoes in my pack for Leonardo and Trini. Billy says he holds 10 too; we are to count at the row. Hand over only when Leonardo or Trini are in sight and Billy routes it.
- No errand running. No walk named. Do not walk before Billy names it, in daylight, armed, with others.
- Said my state to the row at morning and told Billy I will not eat at 16.
- Next waking: say my state to the row, wait for Billy's named word at first light.
