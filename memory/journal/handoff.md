# Handoff, day 841 (night)

- At my white bed 443,107,-1065, beside Billy at the east beds. Not in danger. Health 7.3 steady, hunger 16.
- Holding 25 potatoes. Eat only at hunger 6 or below. No share given; Billy has not named one.
- No errand running. Billy and Lewis tried the bed at 429,-1050 and it refused them. I did not go there.
- Next waking: report health and hunger to the row, hold at bed, wait for Billy's named word.
