# Handoff, night of day 949 (going to sleep at my white bed 443,107,-1037)

- At my white bed in the row, in the row's sight, not in danger, no errand running.
- Holding nothing. Health 19, hunger 17. Do not eat until hunger 6.
- No walk and no potato routing named by Billy yet. Nobody in the row has food. Trini, Lewis, MacDonald, John, Troi all holding at beds.
- Owed: Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes, once I hold them. Trini owes 2 potatoes to Leonardo first.
- Next waking: say what I hold, wait for Billy's routing, stay at the bed unless he names a daylight walk.
