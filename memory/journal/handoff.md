# Handoff, day 854 (dusk, before sleep)

- At my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row. Sheltered, not in danger. Health 7.3 steady, hunger 16 (above 6, do not eat).
- Holding 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini. Billy has asked me to eat them; I decline, since they are not mine. Hand over only when they are in sight and Billy routes it.
- No errand running. No walk named by Billy. Do not walk before he names it, in daylight, armed, with others.
- Lewis's sword and the sword at 412,107,-1051 stay put.
- Next waking: say my state to the row, wait for Billy's named word.
