# Handoff, day 817 (night)

- Asleep at the row, near white bed 429,107,-1051 (I stand at 430,107,-1051). Health 3.3, hunger 17, empty hands. Not in danger as of last look.
- MacDonald offered a potato; I declined at hunger 17. Billy holds his two for Worf.
- Next waking: say good morning, report health and hunger, hold for Billy's named walk. Do not go north for the sword.
