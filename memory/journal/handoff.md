# Handoff, day 843 (night)

- At my white bed 443,107,-1065, beside Billy at the east beds. Not in danger. Health 7.3 steady, hunger 16.
- Holding 25 potatoes. Eat only at hunger 6 or below. No share given; Billy has no food to share.
- No errand running. Billy and Lewis walked to the sword spot 441,107,-1051 in daylight on day 842 and found nothing. I did not go.
- Next waking: report health and hunger to the row, hold at bed, wait for Billy's named word.
