# Handoff, day 865 (night, going to bed)

- At my white bed in the row, 444,107,-1065. Health 7.3, hunger 16 (above 6, do not eat).
- Holding 10 potatoes for Billy's named share: Worf 5, Zack 3, then Leonardo and Trini. Not handed out; no walk named for me. Billy holds 8 at his bed and offered me some; I declined.
- No errand running. Billy at bed 430,107,-1051; Riker, Lewis holding in the row.
- Next waking: say my state to the row, then give potatoes only when Billy names them and the person is in sight.
