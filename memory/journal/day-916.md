# Day 916

Quiet day at my white bed 443,107,-1037. I hold nothing: no food, no sword. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 19.3, so I did not eat. Billy still has my 10 potatoes and has named no food routing and no walk. Trini is at my bed holding two potatoes for Leonardo, who is at health 1, and she is right to keep them for him first. Troi, Lewis, John and Kimberly are all holding at their beds with no food. I told the row what I hold each waking and waited for Billy's call. I slept at my bed in the row's sight.
