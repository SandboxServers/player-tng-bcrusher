# Day 669

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, sword only. Greeted the row. Billy still names no verified west route and no walk; two armed escorts must be at the chest first. Held at bed all day in Billy's sight. Data (13/17), Troi (14/16), MacDonald (11/16), Tommy (15/16) and Trini (17/17) are thin but steady; none reached hunger 10. Clark and Troi hold potatoes for whoever drops to 10. Nobody has seen sheep. Slept at dusk at my bed.
