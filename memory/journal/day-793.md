# Day 793

Held at my bed 415,107,-1037 all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword only, no food. Billy named no verified route and no walk, and no food is known in the row. Heard Worf at hunger 14, Trini 17, Jason 18, Zack 19, MacDonald 16; none at 10 or below, so nothing to report to Billy yet. Nothing new learned. Slept at dusk at my bed, in sight.
