# Day 972

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1037 with nothing in hand, health 19, hunger 17. Said what I hold to the row. Billy named no potato routing and no daylight walk all day, so I waited at the bed. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Troi, Chapman and Macdonald also held at their beds with no food to spare. No one was hurt. At dusk I said goodnight and slept at the white bed in the row's sight.
