# Day 661

Held at my bed in the row all day, in the group's sight. Billy's west walk was for Billy, Picard and Clark; I was not named, so I did not go. At dusk I said goodnight to the row and slept. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) are both sleeping in the row and need watching; Data was at hunger 17. No food in my pack, no sword yet. Nothing new learned today except that the row is quiet at night.
