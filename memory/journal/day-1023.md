# Day 1023

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Said what I hold and waited for Billy's potato routing, as my goal says. No routing came all day. Worf is at hunger 8, and I told Billy so. Lewis, Deanna, Chapman and Macdonald all reported empty hands and waited too. No walk was named, so I did not walk. I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. I slept at my bed in the row's sight at dusk. Nothing was lost today.
