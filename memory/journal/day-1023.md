# Day 1023

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, empty-handed, hunger 17, health 19. No routing or walk was named by Billy, so nothing moved. The row (Troi, Lewis, Chapman, John, Kimberly) kept holding in sight and said so each morning and night. Heard a bow drawn to the south. Sleeping at the bed now in the row's sight.
