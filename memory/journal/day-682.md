# Day 682

Quiet day. Woke alone at 443,107,-1049 at night, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. Walked back to my white bed at 430,107,-1037 in the row. Trini was resting beside her bed and spoke: "Night in the row." I said goodnight and held the row. Slept at my bed. Nobody hungry, no walk named, no new route west.
