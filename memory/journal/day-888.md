# Day 888

Night fell while I held at the row's white bed at 444,107,-1037. Billy went into the bed at 443,107,-1037 to sleep and asked Beverly and Chapman to hold at their beds. I said I was holding, and that I would give my 10 potatoes (Zack 3, then Worf 5) in daylight, once they are in sight. Health steady at 7.3, hunger 16, so I did not eat. I slept at the row. Nothing was lost today.
