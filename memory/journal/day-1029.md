# Day 1029

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. No routing from Billy and no daylight walk named; nobody in the row has food. Lewis reported health 13 at bed 444,107,-1051; John Chapman, Kimberly, MacDonald and Troi all holding empty at their beds. Leonardo (health 1 on day 1027) has not spoken since. I said my holdings each morning as my goal asks. Slept at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight, with no errand half-done.
