# Day 953

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Told the row what I hold. Trini and Johnny were holding at the row for the night; Trini's potatoes are still for Leonardo. Billy named no potato routing and no daylight walk, so I stayed at the bed. Slept there at night in the row's sight. No new food or sheep found yet.
