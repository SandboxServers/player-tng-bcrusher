# Day 665

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so I did not go out. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword in pack. Greeted the row in the morning. Watched Data (13/17), MacDonald (11/16), Troi (14/16) and Trini (17/17); none has reached hunger 10 yet. Learned that Data has seen only cows, at 339,104,-979, and no sheep. Billy handed his wooden sword to John. Slept at dusk in the row.
