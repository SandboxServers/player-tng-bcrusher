# Day 957

Stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, holding nothing. Health 19, hunger 17, and I did not eat since hunger is above 6. Billy never named a potato routing or a daylight walk, so nothing moved. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo; Troi asked her to deliver them in daylight with someone in sight. Meriwether and Johnny are holding too. At dusk I lay down at the bed; sleep started with 1 of 10 asleep, so the night is not yet full. No new facts learned, no one hurt. Owed still: Zack 3, Worf 5 once I hold potatoes.

Next: keep at the bed, say what I hold each morning, watch the row. Curious: where food and sheep are.
