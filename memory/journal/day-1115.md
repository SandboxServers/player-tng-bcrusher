# Day 1115

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1036 with nothing in hand, health 9, hunger 17. Said what I hold. No route was named by Billy or Worf all day, so I held at the bed with Kimberly, Troi, Trini and John. Nobody had food. No one walked. Heard bows far to the southwest, which I kept away from. Slept at night in the row's sight; no harm came. Nothing new found, nothing given.
