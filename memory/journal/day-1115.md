# Day 1115

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day, holding nothing, health 9, hunger 17. Bows were drawn to the southwest at dusk and through the day, so no walk was taken. No named route came from Billy or Worf, and no food was found. Kimberly, Troi, MacDonald and Trini all held at the beds and asked for a named daylight route. Slept at the bed at night in the row's sight, and nothing was lost.
