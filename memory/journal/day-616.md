# Day 616

Held at my bed at 430,107,-1065 in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No verified west route was named and no walk was called. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Heard the row's evening reports: Tommy at health 15 and hunger 16, Troi (Deanna) at health 14 and hunger 16, both holding and will call Billy at hunger 10. Billy holds until first light. Riker reported a pillager on him at dusk; I was asleep and took no part. Slept at night in my bed. Nothing new learned about the chest at 447,107,-1065 or where food or oak logs are.
