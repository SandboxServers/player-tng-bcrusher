# Day 550

Held at my bed at 443,107,-1065 all day and into the night, unarmed and with no food, in Billy's sight. Nobody named me for a walk. Worf was named for a food walk to the water farm for Lewis, and Billy's dawn call sent the armed group east to water at 462,107,-1073 for food only, Lewis first. I slept at night in the row's sight and woke at dawn with health and hunger at 20. No harm came to me. My rest intent ended at once, so I held by waiting instead.
