# Day 978

Night at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row. Held nothing, health 19, hunger 17 (not eating until 6). Billy had not named a potato routing or a daylight walk, so I did not move. Said what I hold aloud. Slept at the bed; sleep began with 1 of 10 needed asleep. No one hurt me.
