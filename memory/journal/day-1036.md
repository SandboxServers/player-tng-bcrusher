# Day 1036

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day in Billy's row, holding nothing, health 19 to 20, hunger 17. No routing from Billy came. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Worf and Leonardo, and no open cell past 423 was named, so nobody walked. Johnny, Kimberly, MacDonald, Troi and Trini all held at their beds and spoke what they held. I said what I held and waited. Slept at my bed in the row's sight.
