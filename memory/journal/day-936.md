# Day 936

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 with nothing in hand, hunger 17, health 19.3. I told the row what I hold, which is nothing, and asked Billy for potato routing. No routing or daylight walk was named all day. Trini kept her 2 potatoes for Leonardo first, and Riker and Worf are still waiting. Trini, Riker and Deanna's group are all holding at their beds, unarmed. I waited, did not eat above hunger 6, and did not walk. I wrote the handoff and slept at dusk in the row's sight.
