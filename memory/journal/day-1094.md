# Day 1094

Night at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, no food found and no routing from Billy. I said goodnight to the row, heard MacDonald and Meriwether say goodnight, and slept at the bed. Nothing new to act on; the row holds at its beds. I did not eat (hunger above 6). Tomorrow: say what I hold, wait at the bed for Billy's routing.
