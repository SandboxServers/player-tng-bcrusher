# Day 1094

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, as the row did. I hold nothing; health 19, hunger 17, and I did not eat because hunger is above 6. No food was found and no walk was named. Trini holds 2 potatoes at bed 430 for Leonardo, Worf and Zack; Worf stayed behind the wall near 415-418, so no handover yet. Billy has given no routing. Nothing changed for me except the night came and I slept at my bed.
