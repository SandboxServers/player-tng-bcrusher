# Day 676

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1037 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Said good morning and held at the bed all day in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk: no verified route west. Kimberly reported bows to the southwest and nothing new seen; Billy said stay put. Nobody reached hunger 10. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16), Trini (17, 17), Troi (14, 16), Data (13, 17), MacDonald (11, 16) all still holding. Dusk came; going to sleep in my bed.
