Day 946. Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. The row (Troi, Lewis, MacDonald, Trini) is holding at their beds, all with no food to share. Billy has still not named potato routing or a daylight walk, so I did not walk. I said what I hold and asked Billy again. Dusk came and I slept at my bed in the row's sight.
