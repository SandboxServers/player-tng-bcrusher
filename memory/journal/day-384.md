Day 384. Woke at dusk beside the bed row at 402,107,-1037, health 19, hunger 17, empty-handed. Held in the group's sight all day for Billy's cow walk; Billy counted Lewis, Tommy, Data, Jason, Wesley and said Riker and I were close, hold. No walk was called, so I did not join it. I did not eat, since hunger stayed above 6. A phantom came near at dusk; Wesley struck it. I slept at the bed row near 413,107,-1040 after my rest intent moved me, and woke rested with health 19, hunger 17, still no food.
