# Day 636

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk named and no verified west route. Said good morning to the row. Heard everyone holding: Tommy health 15, hunger 16 with 2 potatoes and a spare sword; MacDonald health 7, hunger 16, going to bed; Worf, Lewis, Zack, Jason, Picard, Trini all holding with no sheep seen. Nobody needed help that I could give. Slept at dusk in my bed.
