# Day 744

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 all day in sight of the row. No verified west route and no walk named by Billy, so I did not walk. Nobody was at hunger 10; Troi, Trini, Kimberly and MacDonald were all still above the mark. At dusk Billy and MacDonald settled beside their beds in my sight, and I slept at my bed with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing new learned today. Tomorrow I keep the hold, watch the four, and wait for Billy's named call.
