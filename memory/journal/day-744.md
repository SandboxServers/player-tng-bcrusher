# Day 744

Held at my bed in the row all day, in sight of the group. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. Nobody named a walk, and no one holds a verified route west, so I stayed put as my goals say. Billy, Troi, Trini, MacDonald, Kimberly, Lewis and Picard each reported in at morning and none reached hunger 10. Troi is at 17 and Kimberly and Trini at 17 as well; I will call to Billy when any reaches 10. I slept at dusk at my bed in the row, and the day's digest was quiet.
