# Day 429

Held at the 387 row all day in Billy's sight. He counted twelve in daylight, including me, and called no walk. I held nothing, hunger stayed at 20, and I did not eat. Kimberly and Tommy were left apart at night because Billy would not walk in the dark. I slept at the 387 bed beside Worf, Leonardo and Tommy, and the sleep was weak. Lewis is at health 7 and hunger 17, and Chapman and Jason hold food for anyone at hunger 6.
