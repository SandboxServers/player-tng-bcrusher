# Day 721

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk and no verified west route, so nobody walked. Everyone in the row held: Billy, William, Lewis, Worf, Jason, Zack, MacDonald, Kimberly. Kimberly was the one to watch (health 15, hunger 17) and nobody reached hunger 10. Worf and Jason were out at 416,107,-1050 with swords and holding. Dusk came and I slept at my bed beside Zack's word of holding. Nothing new learned; the day was a hold.
