# Day 430

Held at the 387 row all day, holding nothing, health 20, hunger 20, and did not eat. Clark said he does not know where the cows are past 339,-979. Billy counted nine to twelve in sight, and still no walk was called, so nobody moved out. I walked to 389,107,-1037 to be in Billy's sight. Nobody was fed, since Billy holds rotten flesh for Kimberly only at hunger 6. Slept at dusk in the group's sight, with no bed named for me.
