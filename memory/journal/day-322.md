# Day 322

Woke at the beds with health 15.7 and hunger 17, carrying only 2 bone. Macdonald and Chapman set out for the cows at 368,94,-1118 with Macdonald's sword. I tried to follow him but he was out of sight, so I waited at the beds, then walked to the cow spot myself. No cows were there and dusk came, so I walked back to the beds and slept beside them. Nothing was eaten today. Johnny, Troi and Worf were fed by others. Picard reports that the cow spot at 370,93,-1124 is empty and guarded by skeletons.

People: Macdonald (sword, reliable, leads the cow walks), Chapman/Johnny (hungry, fed by melon today), Picard (chicken and melon, keeps stock for the weak), Troi (melon at the west edge), Worf (3 melon, health 20), Wesley (6 melon, at the beds).
