# Day 598

Held at my bed at 429,107,-1065 all day in Billy's sight. No verified west route and no walk named, so nobody walked. I greeted the row in the morning and reported health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Tommy and Troi are at hunger 16; Tommy at health 15. Neither has reached 10 yet, so nothing to report to Billy. Worf, Wesley and Jason still have no spare sword; Tommy's spare is still open to ask after Worf, Picard and Wesley are served. At dusk the row went to bed; Jason could not get into his bed, and Billy, Picard, Wesley slept in sight. I slept at my bed.
