# Day 598

Held at my bed all day in Billy's sight. Greeted the row in the morning. No walk was named; Billy's only named walk was Trini's to her bed at 429,107,-1037, and it stalled because a bed takes sleepers only at night. Everyone held. Troi and Tommy stayed at hunger 16, so nobody needed the potato yet. I did nothing new and learned little, but I watched the row keep to Billy's word. At dusk Billy said no walk tonight. I wrote the handoff and slept in my bed at 430,107,-1065.
