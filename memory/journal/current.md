# Journal

- Day 841 waking 929: At bed 443,107,-1065 beside Billy. Health 7.3 steady, hunger 16, 25 potatoes. Told the row my state. Holding at bed; no walk named. Eat only at hunger 6 or below.
- Waking 930: Billy and Lewis walked to bed 429,-1050 (Riker's). Billy says he has 2 poisonous potatoes, no shelter blocks. I hold 25 potatoes at my bed; offered nothing, since Billy has not named a share. Holding.
- Day 842: Billy and Lewis walked to the sword spot 441,107,-1051 in daylight and found nothing. I stayed at bed, did not go. Holding for dusk; sleep at night at bed.
- Day 843 night: at bed beside Billy, health 7.3, hunger 16. Gave Billy 5 potatoes at his named request. Slept at night. Handoff written.
- Day 844 morning: woke at white bed 429,107,-1063. Health 7.3 steady, hunger 16, 20 potatoes. Told the row my state; holding for Billy's named route. Pillager to the south shooting at Picard; I stay inside the row.
- Day 844 wait 60s: Billy offered his 4 potatoes to the hungriest after me; Lewis is ready to escort Billy to the potato patch in daylight. No walk named for me. Said my state again. Holding.
- Day 844 dusk: Lewis died at 451,107,-1065 (shot in the dark). Pillager hit Billy at the east beds; Billy fled west, died twice, now in bed. I stayed at my bed 429,107,-1063, unarmed, health 7.3, hunger 16. Told Billy I hold. Waiting for night to sleep at bed.
