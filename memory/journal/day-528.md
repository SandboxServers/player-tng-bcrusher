# Day 528

Quiet day at the row. At dawn I walked to the chest at 447,107,-1065 and opened it; the open worked, but I still cannot see what it holds. No count from Billy. Riker still holds my wooden sword in his pack and waits for Billy's call. Pack empty, health and hunger 20. I told the group aloud that I hold nothing. Went to my bed at 443,107,-1065 at dusk, in sight, and slept.
