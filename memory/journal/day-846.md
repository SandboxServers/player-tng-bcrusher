# Day 846

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 all day, health 7.3 steady, hunger 16, 20 potatoes. Told the row my state each waking. Billy asked me to bring the potatoes to the chest at 445,107,-1065 in daylight, with me first, then Troi and Leonardo. I did not walk without a named route and escort; I said I would go at first light if he names it. Leonardo is at health 10, hunger 17, and Troi has no food. Dusk came with thunder; I slept at my bed. Hunger has not reached 6, so I have not eaten.
