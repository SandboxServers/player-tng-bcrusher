# Day 933

Held still at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day and night, sheltered, holding nothing. Health 19.3, hunger 17; I did not eat, as my rule says (hunger 6 or below). Billy named no walk and no potato routing, so I did not walk. I said good night and good morning to the row, told Troi how I was, and watched the others hold at their beds. Riker is at health 11.3 with no food. Trini, at health 11 earlier, is still on my watch list. I went to sleep at my bed as night fell.
