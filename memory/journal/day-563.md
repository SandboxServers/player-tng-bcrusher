# Day 563

Held at my white bed at 443,107,-1065 all day in the row's sight, unarmed, with no food. Billy did not name a walk for me. Potatoes went from Tommy to Deanna, and Jason carried Wesley's 8 to him near 415,107,-1047 with Billy in sight. I said a word to the row at night. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Slept at dusk at my bed.
