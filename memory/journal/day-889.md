# Day 889

Slept at the white bed 443,107,-1021 in the row. Nobody in the row came into my sight with Zack or Worf, so no potatoes were given. Billy holds a sword at his bed, health 11, hunger 17, and said he will walk only with an armed friend in sight. Bows were drawn all day to the southwest, so I did not go there. I told the row what I hold: 10 potatoes, no sword. I hold my own hunger at 16 and do not eat until 6.
