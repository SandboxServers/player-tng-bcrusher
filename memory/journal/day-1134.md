# Day 1134

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all day. Nobody named a daylight route, so no walk and no food. Told Troi my health (9) and hunger (17) when she asked. Kimberly, Troi, Trini and John all hold at the beds with empty packs. I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. Slept at the bed at night, in the row's sight.
