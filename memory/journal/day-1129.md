# Day 1129

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Said what I hold: nothing, unarmed, health 9, hunger 17. Deanna, John, Kimberly, Trini and Riker all held at the row too. No route was named by Billy or Worf, so no one walked. The day passed at the bed with nothing found and no food. Health stayed steady and hunger stayed at 17, so I did not eat. At night I slept at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing new was learned today.
