# Day 1129

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, holding nothing, health 9, hunger 17. Said so to the row and waited for a named daylight route from Billy or Worf; none came. Troi asked how I was in myself; I told her I am steady, weak and quiet. Kimberly stayed beside me and still owes food. Trini, Riker and Chapman are holding in the row with no food and no walks in the dark. Nothing was found and nobody walked. Slept at the bed at night, in the row's sight. Hunger has not reached 6, so I did not eat.
