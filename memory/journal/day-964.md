# Day 964

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 with health 19, hunger 17, holding nothing. I said so to the row and held at the bed all day. Billy has not named potato routing or a daylight walk. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack, and is at health 11. Deanna, Kimberly, MacDonald, Lewis and Trini all reported holding still and unarmed. Nothing was given or eaten. At dusk I slept at the bed in the row's sight. No deaths, no damage.
