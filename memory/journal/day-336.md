# Day 336

Woke at the beds with 17 melon, health 19, hunger 17. Nobody weak came into sight. Walked east with Data, Worf and Chapman to look for sheep; the way east at 472,107 was blocked, and no sheep were found. Turned back at dusk to the beds and slept beside the bed at 402,107,-1081. Data and Lewis are close by, and the group is steady.
