# Day 664

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health 20, hunger 20, no food; I ate nothing. The sword from Billy is in my pack. At dusk Billy, Picard, Clark's William, Worf, MacDonald and Data all settled to sleep in the row. Data (health 13, hunger 17), MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are still the ones to watch. I slept at my bed at night and nothing new happened.
