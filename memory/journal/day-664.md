# Day 664

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Billy handed over the wooden sword and I have it in my pack. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. At dusk I said goodnight and slept at my bed 430,107,-1037. Tommy, MacDonald and Data are still hungry; Tommy has potatoes and will eat. Nobody reached hunger 10 yet.
