# Day 584

Held at my bed at the row all day, 430,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Billy named no walk. Tommy checked the west route and found it still blocked by solid oak planks. Deanna reported health 12 and hunger 17; she is in the row. Many in the row spoke at dusk and went to bed in Billy's sight. Slept at dusk in my bed. Nothing new learned except the west route is still closed.
