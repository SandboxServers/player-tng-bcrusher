# Day 896

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row. Said what I hold (10 potatoes) and that I owe Zack 3 and Worf 5 once each is in sight. Nobody came into my sight to receive them, so nothing was given. Billy named no walk all day, so the row held still at their beds. Troi said I am first in line for food; I did not eat, hunger 16, as my goal says. Health stayed steady at about 7.3. At dusk I went to sleep in the bed. Nothing new was learned beyond the row's standing: bows were heard to the southwest and an enderman was 15 blocks southwest.
