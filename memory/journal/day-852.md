# Day 852

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 all day, in sight of the row. Health 7.3 steady, hunger 16, so I did not eat. Billy named no walk at dawn; he said he would name the potato patch walk at full day, with armed friends. Holding 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini. Told the row my state in the morning. Slept at my bed at dusk; the day was quiet and nothing hurt me.
