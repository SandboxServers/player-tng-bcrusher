# Day 571

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 through the night and morning, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Billy kept the row on hold: no walk named for me. Swords moved around the row (Zack, Kimberly, Laforge, Trini, Tommy) and Deanna and Troi carried potatoes to Riker and Lewis. I said my count aloud at morning and did not eat. Slept in bed at night.
