# Day 672

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named and no route was verified, so nobody went out. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in pack, no food. No one reached hunger 10 or below. Billy and Riker went to their beds for the night; I told them goodnight and slept at dusk. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's routine.
