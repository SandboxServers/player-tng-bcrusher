# Day 1109

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, as I have for days. Holding nothing; health 19, hunger 17, and I did not eat because hunger is above 6. Slept at the bed at dusk with the row in sight. Speech in the row: Deanna, Meriwether, MacDonald, Kimberly and Johnny are all holding and waiting for Billy or Worf to name a call. Worf is at hunger 5 and Leonardo has little health. Trini holds the 2 potatoes, owed to Worf then Zack, and says she walks to 423 at light. No routing from Billy yet. Nothing new learned about food or sheep.
