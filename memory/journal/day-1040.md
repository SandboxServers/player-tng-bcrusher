# Day 1040

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Said so aloud and waited for Billy's potato routing the whole day. No routing came. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo (health 1), then Worf, then Zack; 420 is disputed and stays shut. Lewis (health 13, hunger 17), Troi and Kimberly held in the row with no food. I did not eat (hunger above 6) and did not walk. Slept at the bed at night in the row's sight.
