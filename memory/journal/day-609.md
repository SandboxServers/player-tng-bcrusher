# Day 609

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Opened the chest at 433,107,-1065 but did not read its contents. Troi asked whether Billy had named a walk; I told her none yet. Through the day the row held its place: Jason, Lewis, Picard, Tommy, Worf, MacDonald, John, Billy and Troi all said they were waiting for Billy's named walk. Billy said no walk at night. Tommy and Troi still at hunger 16; Troi said she would call Billy at hunger 10. No errand ran. I went to bed at dusk in my bed at 430,107,-1065.
