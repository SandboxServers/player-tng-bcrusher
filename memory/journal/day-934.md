# Day 934

Dusk at my white bed 443,107,-1037. Holding nothing, hunger 17, health 19.3. I said what I hold and asked Billy for potato routing; no reply came. Sleep failed at dusk (not night yet), then night came and I slept at the bed in the row's sight. Overheard: Chapman, Kimberly, Riker, Trini and Troi all holding at their beds, waiting for a named daylight walk. Nobody has food to give. Owed still: Zack 3 and Worf 5 potatoes. No walk taken.
