# Day 934

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all of day 933 and into dusk of 934. Holding nothing, health 19.3, hunger 17, so I did not eat. Said what I hold to the row and told Billy that Trini and Riker are at health 11 with hunger 17. Nobody named a potato routing or a walk, so nobody moved. Deanna, John and Trini all reported holding still with no food. Nothing hurt anyone. I slept at the bed at dusk.
