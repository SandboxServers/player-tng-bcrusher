# Day 736

Woke at the row, health 20, hunger 20. Greeted the group and held at my bed all day. No verified route west was named and no walk was called. Nobody reached hunger 10. Troi (hunger 17, health 14) and MacDonald (hunger 16) are the ones to watch. Bows were drawn often to the south all day, so the danger is still there. At dusk Billy said no walk tomorrow until a route is verified. I went to my bed at 443,107,-1037 and slept.
