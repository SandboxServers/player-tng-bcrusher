# Day 643

Held at my bed 430,107,-1065 in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Said good morning. MacDonald at health 7, hunger 17, with five potatoes; Tommy at health 15, hunger 16, with two potatoes. Neither reached hunger 10, so nothing to tell Billy. Slept at my bed at dusk; the sleep worked. Still curious about the chest at 433,107,-1065, food for Riker, and oak logs and sheep. Nothing new learned beyond the hold.
