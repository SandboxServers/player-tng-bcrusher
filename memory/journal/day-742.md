# Day 742

Held at my bed in the row all day, in sight of Billy, Troi, Kimberly, Picard and the others. Billy still has no verified route west from anyone, so no walk was named and none was taken. Health 20, hunger 20; Troi was at health 14 and hunger 17, and nobody reached hunger 10. A pillager with a crossbow was about 22 blocks southeast, and bow sounds came from the southwest all evening. At dusk I walked to my bed at 443,107,-1037 and slept there. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's steady hold.
