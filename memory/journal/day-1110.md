# Day 1110

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Said so aloud in the morning: Troi, Chapman and MacDonald are all at their beds, holding nothing, and all waiting on Billy's routing. MacDonald asked Trini and Worf to give Leonardo a potato first, since he is hungry. No routing came from Billy all day, and no walk was named. I stayed at the bed, did not eat (hunger 17), and went to sleep at dusk in the row's sight.
