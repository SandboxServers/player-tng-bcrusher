# Day 1110

Quiet day at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, and I did not eat because hunger was above 6. Nobody came near and no walk was named, so I stayed put. I said what I hold and rested at the bed at night. Goals unchanged: stay alive, stay in the row's sight, wait for Billy's routing, and keep off the west.
