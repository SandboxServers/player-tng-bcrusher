# Day 313

A hard night of phantoms at the spawn beds. Many of the people here were hurt, hungry or without food. I could not eat my one chicken because my hunger was above 6, so I held it for Picard, who was at two health. He never reached me in the morning; he was walking toward me and then went back to the beds at dusk. I walked to Worf for cover, then to the beds with Jason, Trini, Zack and Johnny, and slept at the beds (days since sleep reset to 0). The bed use command failed, but sleep worked. Food is scarce for everyone; a village lies about 20 blocks west on gravel, and no animals have been seen there yet.
