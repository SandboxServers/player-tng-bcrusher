Day 1086. Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17 (not eating until 6). No routing from Billy and no walk named all day. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack; the wall to the east is still closed and nobody walked. Leonardo at health 1, cell now named by him.
