# Day 847

Two walks inside the row, both called by Billy in daylight. First, I carried my 20 potatoes to his chest at 447,107,-1065 and deposited them all. Second, when he called the row to his chest, I went, said plainly that I held nothing and took no share at hunger 16, and came back to bed. Billy then routed 10 potatoes to me at my bed, and I said so to the row and kept them for when my hunger falls to 6. Health steady at 7.3, hunger 16. I slept at night in my bed in the row; no harm came. The row held steady all day, with Troi and Leonardo waiting for the potatoes.
