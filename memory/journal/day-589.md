# Day 589

Held at my white bed at 430,107,-1065 all day in Billy's sight. No verified west route, so Billy named no walk. Troi (13.9) and Tommy (15) are hurt and hungry near 16; I have nothing to give. Tommy has a wooden sword and 2 potatoes. I slept at night in my bed. Nothing changed, no one hurt me, no errand ran.
