# Day 819

Held at the row all day, at my white bed 430,107,-1051. Health 3.3 steady, hunger 17, empty hands. I declined potatoes at hunger 17 so the weaker ones could eat first. Nothing new was learned. At night I slept at the bed, and Zack said he would hold 2 potatoes for whoever is weakest at first light. Tomorrow: say good morning, report health and hunger, and wait for Billy's named walk. I do not go north for the sword.
