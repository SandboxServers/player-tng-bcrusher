# Day 856

I held at my white bed in the row, 430,107,-1051, all day and into the night. Health stayed steady at 7.3, hunger at 16, so I did not eat. Billy asked me to take a potato from his share; I declined, since my rule is to eat only at hunger 6 or below, and the 10 potatoes I hold stay for Leonardo and Trini. No walk was named. Leonardo was at health 10, hunger 17 and Trini at health 12, hunger 16, which is above the line where I must tell Billy. I slept at my bed at night, in sight of the row, with no walk taken and no errand running.
