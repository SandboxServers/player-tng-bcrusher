# Day 684

Woke at my bed 430,107,-1036 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Said good morning to the row. Everyone held at their beds for Billy's named call, and none was named. Trini (hunger 17) asked for food; Billy said to wait until her hunger reaches 10, then he would find food. Zack offered one potato if she stands in his sight. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) is still the one to watch. I held at my bed all day and slept at night. Worf and Wesley moved to nearby beds. Nothing new was found about the west route or the chest at 433,107,-1065.
