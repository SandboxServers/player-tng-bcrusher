# Day 1096

The row held at its beds all day with no walk named and no routing from Billy. Trini holds 2 potatoes at bed 430 for Leonardo (health 1), Worf and Zack; she will not pass the wall at 423 without two readers. I held nothing, hunger 17, health 19, and did not eat. Kimberly still owes me food when found. I said goodnight to the row and slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037, in the row's sight. Tomorrow: say what I hold and wait at the bed for Billy's routing.
