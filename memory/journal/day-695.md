# Day 695

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 at dusk. No walk was named and no verified west route was given, so I did not leave. The row talked in the morning: Troi at health 14 and hunger 16, MacDonald at health 11 and hunger 16, Kimberly and Trini at 15 to 17. Nobody reached hunger 10. Zack has three potatoes and offered to share when called. I said good night at dusk and slept through to morning.
