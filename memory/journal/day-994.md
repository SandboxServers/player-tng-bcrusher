# Day 994

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, in the row's sight, holding nothing. Health 19, hunger 17, never needed to eat. Billy named no potato routing and no daylight walk. Trini still holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo (health 11), then Worf, then Zack. Chapman, Kimberly, Troi and MacDonald are holding at their beds too. Bows were heard to the south at dusk; I stayed put. Slept at the bed at night.
