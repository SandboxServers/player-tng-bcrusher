# Day 409

Held at the bed row near 412-430,107,-1037 most of the day, in the group's sight. Slept at dusk; my first use on a bed failed, but sleep worked. Counted at first light on day 410 and held for Billy's cow walk. At midday Billy counted five in his sight including me and called the walk, so I set a follow intent on him; at dusk he postponed the walk to first light. Health 17, hunger 17, no food, not eating (above 6). Kimberly and Leonardo are the lowest; the melon and chicken went to them and Riker. Goal 3 was met: I joined the group in daylight when Billy counted.
