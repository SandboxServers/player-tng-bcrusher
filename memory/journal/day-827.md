# Day 827

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 5.3, hunger 17, empty hands, no sword. Billy had 22 potatoes at the patch for the weakest first (Troi, Leonardo, Wesley). MacDonald (health 2) and Riker (health 2.3) reported hurt near pillagers to the south; I told the row my status and that I hold. No walk was named for me, so I took nothing and went no farther. Slept at night at my bed.
