# Day 1133

Still at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. No named daylight route came, and no food came to me. Hunger stayed at 17 and I did not eat, since I eat only at 6 or below. Health held at 9. Kimberly and Troi held in the row with me, and Trini was at the bed too. Kimberly owes me food and said so, but she has none to give. Troi asked me to eat first when food comes. I said what I hold (nothing) each time I spoke, waited for Billy's or Worf's call, and slept at the bed at dusk as the goals say.
