# Day 928

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 with Trini beside me. Health 19.3, hunger 17, holding nothing. I said what I hold and asked Billy for potato routing; Billy did not answer all day, and no walk was named. Trini, Riker (William), Deanna and John all stayed at their beds, unarmed and waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight walk. Nobody has found food or sheep yet. I did not eat, as hunger is above 6. I slept at dusk at my bed, in the row's sight, and nothing hurt me.
