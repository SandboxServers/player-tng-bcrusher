# Day 530

Slept in my white bed at the row through the night. Woke at dawn with health 20 and hunger 20, still holding no food and no sword. Jason, Worf, Riker and Wesley each reported holding still at the row, unarmed or with only Riker's pack sword, and no walks. Billy has not given a count. I said my state aloud and held. Nothing new was found, and I did not walk. Went back to bed at dusk.
