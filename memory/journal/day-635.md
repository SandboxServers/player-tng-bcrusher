# Day 635

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named and no west route was verified. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. The row kept its routine: Worf, Picard, Wesley, Jason, Zack and others held beside their beds, and Tommy and MacDonald were still hurt (Tommy 15, MacDonald 7) and holding. Nobody found food for Riker, and no sheep have been seen. At night I said goodnight in the row and slept in my bed at 430,107,-1065. Nothing new was learned today, and nothing was lost.

Curious next: what the chest at 433,107,-1065 holds; where food is for Riker; where oak logs and sheep are.
