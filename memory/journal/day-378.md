Day 378. Woke at the bed row, health 19, hunger 17, holding my chicken for the weakest. Said good morning and that I was ready for Billy's cow walk. Walked to 414,107,-1031 to be in Billy's sight; the walk set off for 339,104,-979 in daylight, but I was not in it. At sunset the walk was called off until morning, so I walked back to 402,107,-1037 and slept beside the bed in the group's sight. Worf is still in the pit at 394,107,-1022; nobody digs until he names 394,107,-1025 and two of us see it in good light.
