# Day 992

Stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 all day in the row's sight. Holding nothing; health 19, hunger 17, did not eat. Billy never named a potato routing or a daylight walk. Trini promised her potatoes to Leonardo first, and Troi reported Leonardo at health 1; I told Billy. Chapman, Kimberly, MacDonald and Troi all held at their beds. Nothing new to act on, so I slept at the bed and wrote the handoff.
