# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I sleep at a white bed in the row, at 444,107,-1065 (bed beside me at 443). Health 7.3, hunger 16 at close of day 870. Holding 10 potatoes for Billy's named share (Worf 5, Zack 3, then Leonardo and Trini).

## Recent days
