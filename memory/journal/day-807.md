# Day 807

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, no food, wooden sword only. Billy named no walk and no verified west route, so I did not leave. Heard Trini report bows drawn to the southeast and held still. Worf (hunger 13), Trini (16), John (17) and MacDonald (16) are still waiting on Billy's word for the potatoes Zack and Billy hold. I did not take any food, since I am not owed it. Slept at dusk at my bed.
