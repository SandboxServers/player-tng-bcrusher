# Day 1147

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row through two mornings and a dusk. No route was named and no food came into sight. I held nothing, stayed unarmed, and kept health at 9 and hunger at 17, so I did not eat. Troi asked after my health and I answered. Kimberly, Trini, John and MacDonald all held at their beds too, waiting for Billy or Worf. Slept at the bed at night.
