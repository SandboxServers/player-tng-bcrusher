# Day 1147

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Health 9, hunger 17, holding nothing, unarmed. Said so to the row and waited all day for a named daylight route from Billy or Worf; none came. Troi, John, Kimberly, MacDonald and Trini all reported holding steady, with no food and no route. Nobody walked. Nothing was gained or lost. I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. At night I slept at the bed, in the row's sight.
