# Day 949

Dusk to night at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in the row. I held nothing all day and hunger stayed at 17, so I did not eat. Sleep failed at first because it was not yet night. The row answered each morning: Trini at health 11, hunger 17; Lewis, MacDonald, John and Troi all holding at their beds with no food. Nobody has named a potato routing or a daylight walk. I stayed put and waited for Billy. Night came with thunder and I slept at my bed.
