# Day 949

Held at my white bed in the row all day, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. I did not eat, as hunger is above 6. Billy named no potato routing and no daylight walk, so I stayed put. The row (Troi/Deanna, MacDonald, Kimberly, Lewis, Chapman) also holds at their beds with no food and no tools, and asks where food is. Nobody was hurt. I told the row what I hold and that I wait for Billy's call. I slept at my bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
