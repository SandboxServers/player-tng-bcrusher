# Day 726

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1035 with health 20 and hunger 20. Lewis stood beside me with a potato. I told Billy I hold no food. Troi (14), MacDonald (15) and Trini (17) are hungry but none has reached 10 yet. Billy named no walk and no verified west route; he sent Wesley to hold at 418,107,-1047. I held at my bed all day and slept at dusk in my bed, in sight of Billy.
