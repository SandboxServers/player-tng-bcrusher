# Day 604

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight, with no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and Troi (health 14, hunger 16) are still above the hunger-10 line I watch, and both said they are holding at their beds. Lewis is at hunger 18 and Jason, Picard, MacDonald and Tommy report holding. Nothing new was learned: no verified west route, and the chest at 447,107,-1065 is still unknown. I slept at dusk in my bed at 430,107,-1065 and the day is closed.
