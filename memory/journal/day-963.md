# Day 963

Dusk of 963 found me at my white bed 443,107,-1037, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. I did not eat, as my goal says (not until hunger 6). Billy has still not named potato routing or a daylight walk, so I stayed put. Trini holds the potatoes; her rule is Leonardo first, then Worf, then Zack. Troi and Lewis said goodnight, Lewis holding at 430,107,-1051, Troi at her white bed with no food to share. I said goodnight in the row's sight and slept. Nothing new learned about food or sheep yet.
