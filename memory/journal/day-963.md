# Day 963

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Said so aloud at first light. Billy still has not named potato routing or a daylight walk, so I waited at the bed the whole day. Trini holds 2 potatoes (health 11) for Leonardo, Worf and Zack. The row (Troi, MacDonald, John, Meriwether, Kimberly, Lewis) is holding too, with no food and no walk called. Signs at the row mark shot deaths on Day 1061. Nothing new was learned. I went to bed at night at my bed, in the row's sight, not in danger.
