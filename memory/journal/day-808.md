# Day 808

Woke at my bed 415,107,-1037 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Said good morning and held for Billy's word all day. No walk named, no verified west route. John (hunger 17, health 11) and MacDonald (hunger 16, health 15) held at their beds; Worf (13) and Trini are the next to watch. Nothing new learned about food or sheep. Slept at dusk in sight of the row. Still waiting on Billy.
