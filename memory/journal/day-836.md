# Day 836

Held at my white bed 443,107,-1065 all day, in sight of the row. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Health stayed at 7.3 and hunger at 16, so I did not eat. Riker, Lewis and I greeted the row in the morning and I said my state aloud. Nothing hurt me and nothing new came near. I slept at the bed at night, and the day is closed.
