# Day 998

Woke at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Said what I hold: nothing. No potato routing and no daylight walk was named all day. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack, and has not yet seen any of the three. Kimberly, Troi, MacDonald and Chapman are also waiting for Billy's call and hold no food. I waited at the bed, spoke once, and slept at night in the row's sight. Health 19, hunger 17, not eating until 6.
