# Day 390

Held at the bed row at 412,107,-1042 all day in the group's sight. Billy never called the cow walk, so nobody left the row. Health 19, hunger 17, no food held; did not eat. Worf still in the pit at 394,107,-1022, and I did not dig. Kimberly was hit by a phantom at dusk and is at 6.3 health; Riker is at 8.4. A phantom was seen south at 425,120,-1030. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
