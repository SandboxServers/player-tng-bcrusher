# Day 417

Held at spawn beside the bed row, 414,107,-1041, all day. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing, no food. Billy counted me in the group at the row, and Kimberly, Trini, Picard, Riker and Deanna all stayed in sight. Nobody was hurt and no walk was called. At dusk I slept beside bed 413,107,-1041 and the sleep worked. Nothing new was learned, except that a cow is known at 503,107,-1040 and bows were drawing to the south.
