# Day 1068

Quiet day at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, not eating until 6. No potato routing from Billy yet; Trini still holds 2 potatoes at 430,107,-1051 for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Heard the row say goodnight: Chapman, Kimberly and Troi all holding at their beds with no food. Signs in the row mark earlier deaths by shooting on day 1061 (Kimberly's and MacDonald's). I slept at my bed in the row's sight.
