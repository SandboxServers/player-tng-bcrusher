# Day 621

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Billy sent Tommy with the spare sword toward Wesley; the path was blocked again and everyone turned back. No walk for me. MacDonald was at health 7 and Troi urged him to eat. I stayed at health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Slept at dusk in my bed at 430,107,-1065 with the row around me.
