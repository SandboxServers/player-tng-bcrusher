# Day 621

Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. Billy's west try with Tommy was blocked again, so no walk came for me. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and Troi (hunger 16) are still watched; MacDonald is at health 7, hunger 16, and says he will eat at 10. At dusk the row settled for the night and I slept in my white bed at 430,107,-1065.
