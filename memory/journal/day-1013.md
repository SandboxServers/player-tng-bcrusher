# Day 1013

Night at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, so I did not eat (hunger above 6). No routing from Billy and no daylight walk named. I said what I hold aloud, waited at the bed, then slept. Four of ten needed were asleep when I lay down. Nothing new was learned today. Still curious where food and sheep are. Tell Billy and Trini when they are near.
