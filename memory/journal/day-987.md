# Day 987

Stayed at my white bed 443,107,-1037 the whole day, holding nothing, health 19, hunger 17, and did not eat (hunger above 6). Billy gave no potato routing and no daylight walk, so I did not move. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack; she is at health 11. MacDonald, Troi and Johnny (Chapman) are all at their beds in the row, waiting for a named call. Nothing hurt me. At night I slept at my bed, in the row's sight.
