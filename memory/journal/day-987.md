# Day 987

Slept at my white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row. Holding nothing all day; health 19, hunger 17, I did not eat. Billy never named a potato routing or a daylight walk, so I waited at the bed with Trini, Johnny, Troi, Kimberly and MacDonald. Troi reported Leonardo at health 1; I told Billy and restated that Trini's two potatoes go to Leonardo first. Nothing was given and no one walked.
