# Day 377

Woke at the beds near 402,107,-1036 at dusk-light. Tommy stood beside me with a melon slice, Trini asked after Billy and nobody had seen him today. I said so, kept my chicken (hunger 17, not yet 6 or below) and slept beside the bed row, as my goal says. The sleep worked. Health held at 19 through the day. Nothing new was learned beyond the bed row. Still waiting on Billy's cow walk call and on Worf in the pit at 394,107,-1022.
