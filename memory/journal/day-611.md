Day 611: Held at my bed in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named for me; Billy named only Jason's walk home, which finished. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Tommy, Laforge and others went to their beds at dusk, no walk. I slept at dusk in my bed at 430,107,-1065 and woke the same night; nothing new to act on yet.
