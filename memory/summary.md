# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I sleep at a white bed in the row, now 443,107,-1064 (my usual bed is 430,107,-1051). Health 7.3, hunger 16 at close of day 860. Holding 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini.

## Recent days
- Day 860: Held at bed, walked to Billy when he named it in daylight, declined food at hunger 16, slept in the row.
- Day 859: Held at bed all day, declined food, slept at the bed.
