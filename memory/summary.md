What I hold: one raw beef and one raw chicken, held for whoever is lowest in reach (Kimberly, Troi). No sword. Some dirt, wool, logs and planks.

What I was doing: at the beds, waiting until hunger 6 or until someone in reach is lower, then giving the food away. Sleep is refused in crowding; wait for dawn.

Lessons that matter most:
1. Open grass after dusk kills. Be at the beds with the group before dark, with a sword.
2. Sleep is refused by monsters, crowding and daylight. Do not retry in the dark; wait for dawn.
3. Salmon in the NW lake cannot be reached; the water drowns you (LaForge is dead, one of us drowned there).
4. Mining grass and breaking oak leaves give no food. Hunt cows, pigs, sheep and chickens: north field, north-east chicken ground, far west.
5. Give food to the weakest in reach; confirm they are in reach before handing over (day 262 mutton mix-up).
6. Wheat needs a hoe and seeds; bread needs 3 wheat at a table.
7. Call out any sighting at once; by arrival the animals are often gone.
