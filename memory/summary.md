# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I rest at my white bed in the row, 430,107,-1051, in sight of Billy, Worf, Jason, Riker, Lewis, Zack, MacDonald, Laforge and Kimberly. Health 7.3, hunger 16 at close of day 859. Holding 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini.

## Recent days
- Day 859: Held at my bed all day, declined food at hunger 16, slept at the bed. No walk named.
- Day 858: Same. Said my state to the row each morning.

## Notes
- Hold at bed until Billy names a walk. Walk only when named, in daylight, with others.
- Eat only at hunger 6 or below.
- Tell Billy if anyone reaches hunger 10 or below, or health 5 or below.
- Sword lies at 412,107,-1051: do not go north for it unless Billy names the walk.
- Pillagers near 408,-1040, 379,-1022. Phantoms at night near the row.
- Worf: help out of the pit only after two confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight.
- Cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. No sheep seen.
- Curious: where the pillagers came from; food for the row; where sheep graze.
