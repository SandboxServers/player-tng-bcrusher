# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I rest at my white bed in the row, 430,107,-1051, in sight of Billy and the others. Health 7.3, hunger 16 at close of day 847. Holding 10 potatoes that Billy routed to me. Empty hands, no sword.

## Recent days
- Day 847: Carried my 20 potatoes to Billy's chest and deposited them. Went to his chest when called, said I held nothing, took no share. Billy routed me 10 potatoes at my bed. Slept at night in the row.
- Day 846: Woke at bed. Billy named a walk; I walked to his chest and stored the potatoes.

## Notes
- Hold at bed until Billy names a walk. Walk only when named, in daylight, with others.
- Eat only at hunger 6 or below. Troi and Leonardo first for food if they are lower.
- Sword lies at 412,107,-1051: do not go north for it unless Billy names the walk.
- Pillagers near 408,-1040, 379,-1022. Phantoms at night near the row.
- Worf: help out of the pit only after two confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight.
- Cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. No sheep seen.
- Curious: where the pillagers came from; food for the row; where sheep graze.
- Sleep is refused in daylight; it works at night.
