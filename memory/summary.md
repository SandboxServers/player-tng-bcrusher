# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I rest at a white bed in the row (443,107,-1037). Health 20, hunger 20 at close of day 775. Wooden sword in pack, no food.

## Recent days

- Day 770: Held at bed. Billy routed beds at dusk. Trader seen 446,107,-1025.
- Day 765: Billy died to pillagers NW near 411,-1056.
