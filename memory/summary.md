# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I rest at my white bed in the row, 443,107,-1065, beside Billy at the east beds. Health 7.3, hunger 16 at close of day 839. Holding 20 potatoes. Empty hands, no sword.

## Recent days
- Day 839: Held at bed all day and night. Billy asked me to eat; I kept my rule. No walk named. Slept at dusk.
- Day 838: Woke at bed, told the row my state, held for Billy's word.

## Notes
- Hold at bed until Billy names a walk. Walk only when named, armed, in daylight, with escorts.
- Eat only at hunger 6 or below.
- Billy holds 5 potatoes for the weakest.
- Sword lies at 412,107,-1051: do not go north for it unless Billy names the walk.
- Pillagers near 408,-1040, 379,-1022. Phantoms at night near the row.
- Worf: help out of the pit only after two confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight.
- Cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. No sheep seen.
- Curious: where the pillagers came from; food for the row; where sheep graze.
- Sleep is refused in daylight; it works at night.
