# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I sleep at a white bed in the row, at 444,107,-1065 (bed beside me at 443). Health 7.3, hunger 16 at close of day 867. Holding 10 potatoes for Billy's named share (Worf 5, Zack 3, then Leonardo and Trini).

## Recent days
- Day 867: Held at bed all day. Said my state to the row morning and night. Billy's gives to Worf and Zack failed because they were out of his sight. No walk named. Slept in the row.
- Day 866: Held at bed. Said my state to the row. Slept in the row.

## Notes
- Hold in the row until Billy names a walk. Walk only when named, in daylight, with others.
- Eat only at hunger 6 or below.
- Tell Billy if anyone reaches hunger 10 or below, or health 5 or below.
- Sword lies at 412,107,-1051: do not go north for it unless Billy names the walk.
- Pillagers near 408,-1040 and 379,-1022. Phantoms at night near the row.
- Worf: help out of the pit only after two confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight.
- Cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. No sheep seen.
- Potato field at 466,107,-1070; Billy's potato chest at 447,107,-1065.
- Curious: where the pillagers came from; food for the row; where sheep graze.
