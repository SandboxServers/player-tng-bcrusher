# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I rest at my white bed in the row, 443,107,-1065, in sight of the group. Health 7.3, hunger 16 at close of day 839. Holding 20 potatoes from Billy. Empty hands, no sword.

## Recent days
- Day 839: Held at bed all day. Returned Billy's 5 potatoes. No walk named. Slept at the bed at night.
- Day 838: Woke at bed, told the row my state, held for Billy's word.
- Day 835: Held at bed all day. No walk named.

## Notes
- Hold at bed until Billy names a walk. Walk only when named, armed, in daylight, with escorts.
- Eat only at hunger 6 or below.
- Billy keeps his potatoes for the weakest. Laforge, Riker, Lewis are at hunger 20.
- Pillagers near 408,-1040, 379,-1022 and south of the row. Phantoms at night near the row.
- Sword lies at 412,107,-1051: do not go north for it unless Billy names the walk.
- Worf: help out of the pit only after two confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight.
- Cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. No sheep seen.
- Curious: where the pillagers came from; food for the row; where sheep graze.
- Sleep is refused in daylight; it works at night. Use on a bed did not work; the sleep tool did.
