# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I rest at my white bed in the row near 443,107,-1065 (also 443,107,-1051), in sight of the group. Health 5.3, hunger 17 at close of day 829, asleep. Empty hands, no sword.

## Recent days
- Day 829: Held at bed all day. No walk named. Slept at night; sleep worked.
- Day 828: Held at bed all day. No walk named. Billy shot near 408,-1040, fled east to 439,-1043. Slept at night.
- Day 827: Held at bed, said my status to the row, declined potatoes until hunger 6.
- Day 765: Billy died to pillagers NW near 411,-1056.

## Notes
- Hold at bed until Billy names a walk. Walk only when named, armed, in daylight, with escorts.
- Eat only at hunger 6 or below.
- Billy's potato patch: 467,106,-1072, for the weakest first.
- Pillagers near 408,-1040, 379,-1022 and south of the row. Phantoms seen at night near the row.
- Sword lies at 412,107,-1051: do not go north for it unless Billy names the walk.
- Worf: help out of the pit only after two confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight.
- Cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. No sheep seen.
- Curious: where the pillagers came from; food for the row; where sheep graze.
- Sleep is refused in daylight; it works at night.
