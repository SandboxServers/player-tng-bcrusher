# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I rest at my white bed 430,107,-1050 in the row, in Billy's sight. Health 3.3, hunger 17 at close of day 818. Empty hands.

## Recent days
- Day 818: Held at bed in the row. Billy offered potatoes; I declined at hunger 17 so Worf and Leonardo ate first. Slept at dusk.
- Day 817: Held at bed. Billy walked to me; declined potato, sent it to Worf.
- Day 765: Billy died to pillagers NW near 411,-1056.

## Notes
- Hold at bed until Billy names a walk for me. Walk only when named, armed, in daylight, with escorts.
- Eat only at hunger 6 or below. Pillagers near 379,-1022.
- Sword lies at 412,107,-1051: do not go north for it unless Billy names the walk.
- Worf: fed by Billy and Zack. Help out of the pit only after two confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight.
- Watch Trini, Worf, Jason, MacDonald, Data, Riker, Troi, Kimberly, Lewis, John Chapman, Wesley, Leonardo; tell Billy if any reaches hunger 10 or below or health 5 or below.
- Cows known at 339,104,-979. No sheep seen.
- Curious: where the pillagers came from; food for Riker and Trini; where sheep graze.
- Could tell: Billy, Zack, Trini, Worf.
- Sleep is refused in daylight; it works at night.
