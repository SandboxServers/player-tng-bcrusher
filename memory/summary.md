# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I sleep at a white bed in the row, at 444,107,-1065 (beside bed 443,107,-1065). Health 7.3, hunger 16 at close of day 862. Holding 10 potatoes; I pass them to whoever is lower when Billy names them (Worf, Zack, then Leonardo and Trini).

## Recent days
- Day 862: Held at bed all day. Riker and Lewis also holding for Billy's named walk. No walk named. Slept in the row.
- Day 861: Held at bed. Billy's potato gives missed Worf and Zack several times; they hold at 417. Slept in the row.

## Notes
- Hold in the row until Billy names a walk. Walk only when named, in daylight, with others.
- Eat only at hunger 6 or below.
- Tell Billy if anyone reaches hunger 10 or below, or health 5 or below. Worf was at 11, no food.
- Sword lies at 412,107,-1051: do not go north for it unless Billy names the walk.
- Pillagers near 408,-1040 and 379,-1022. Phantoms at night near the row.
- Worf: help out of the pit only after two confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight.
- Cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. No sheep seen.
- Curious: where the pillagers came from; food for the row; where sheep graze.
