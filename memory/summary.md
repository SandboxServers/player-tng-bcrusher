# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I rest at my white bed 443,107,-1051 in the row, in Billy's sight. Health 5.3, hunger 17 at close of day 826, asleep. Empty hands, no sword.

## Recent days
- Day 826: Held at bed all night, declined potatoes until hunger 6, no walk named. Slept at night.
- Day 825: Held at bed, told the row my status. Billy reported a phantom near Lewis's bed.
- Day 822: Billy's escorted potato walk went out at dusk without me.
- Day 765: Billy died to pillagers NW near 411,-1056.

## Notes
- Hold at bed until Billy names a walk. Walk only when named, armed, in daylight, with escorts.
- Eat only at hunger 6 or below.
- Pillagers near 379,-1022. Phantoms seen at night near the row.
- Sword lies at 412,107,-1051: do not go north for it unless Billy names the walk.
- Worf: help out of the pit only after two confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight.
- Cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. No sheep seen.
- Curious: where the pillagers came from; food for the row; where sheep graze.
- Sleep is refused in daylight; it works at night.
