# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I rest at my white bed in the row, 430,107,-1051, in sight of Billy, Troi, Jason and the others. Health 7.3, hunger 16 at close of day 848. Holding 10 potatoes that Billy routed to me.

## Recent days
- Day 848: Held at my bed all day. Told the row my state. Billy would not walk to the patch without an armed escort. Lewis's sword at 451,107,-1065 is still there. Slept in the row.
- Day 847: Deposited my potatoes at Billy's chest; he routed 10 back to me at my bed.

## Notes
- Hold at bed until Billy names a walk. Walk only when named, in daylight, with others.
- Eat only at hunger 6 or below. Troi and Leonardo first for food if they are lower.
- Sword lies at 412,107,-1051: do not go north for it unless Billy names the walk.
- Lewis's sword at 451,107,-1065: needs an armed escort named by Billy.
- Pillagers near 408,-1040, 379,-1022. Phantoms at night near the row.
- Worf: help out of the pit only after two confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight.
- Cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. No sheep seen.
- Curious: where the pillagers came from; food for the row; where sheep graze.
- Sleep is refused in daylight; it works at night.
