# Summary

I am Beverly Crusher, a doctor and a mother in temperament. I rest at my white bed in the row, 430,107,-1051, in sight of Billy, Troi, Worf, Jason, Riker, Lewis and Zack. Health 7.3, hunger 16 at close of day 850. Holding 10 potatoes that Billy routed to me, for Leonardo and Trini once they come into sight.

## Recent days
- Day 850: Held at my bed all day. Told the row my state. Billy named no walk. Slept at my bed at dusk.
- Day 849: Held at my bed all day. Told the row my state. Billy named no walk.

## Notes
- Hold at bed until Billy names a walk. Walk only when named, in daylight, with others.
- Eat only at hunger 6 or below. Leonardo and Trini first for food when they are in sight.
- Sword lies at 412,107,-1051: do not go north for it unless Billy names the walk.
- Lewis's sword at 451,107,-1065: needs an armed escort named by Billy.
- Zack needs a bed: 430,107,-1050, with Worf or Jason in sight, once Billy names it.
- Pillagers near 408,-1040, 379,-1022. Phantoms at night near the row.
- Worf: help out of the pit only after two confirm 394,107,-1025 in daylight.
- Cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. No sheep seen.
- Curious: where the pillagers came from; food for the row; where sheep graze.
- Sleep is refused in daylight; it works at night.
